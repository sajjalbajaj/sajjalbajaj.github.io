Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit sajjalbajaj.in, read the blog or contact me about my work. This is the personal portfolio of Sajjal Bajaj, based in Mohali, Punjab, India. It is separate from the websites and privacy practices of my employer and the software providers discussed in my articles.

For questions about this notice or your information, email sajjalbajaj@gmail.com with the subject “Website privacy request”.

Information you choose to share

If you contact me by email, WhatsApp, telephone or LinkedIn, I receive the information you choose to provide. This may include your name, contact details, company, project requirements and attachments. I use it to respond, discuss your enquiry and maintain the correspondence needed for that work.

The website's contact links open the relevant email, phone or messaging service. Their providers also process information under their own privacy policies. Please avoid sending passwords, access tokens, patient records or confidential customer data in an initial enquiry.

Website analytics and guide searches

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people read and how they find and use the site. Analytics may include page addresses, referring websites, device and browser information, approximate location and interaction events.

The site also records selected searches in the Odoo guides directory, including the search text and number of results. Contact click events record the contact method and the area of the page where the link was used. Those custom contact events do not include your message, email address or telephone number. Please do not enter personal or confidential information into guide searches.

Google Analytics currently loads when a page opens; this website does not currently offer a cookie consent panel. Google Analytics can use cookies to distinguish visits. Read Google's explanation of Analytics data safeguards and its cookie documentation.

Cookies and information stored in your browser

  • Appearance preference: local storage remembers your choice of light or dark theme until you clear the site's browser data or change the preference.
  • Portfolio helper: session storage remembers whether the helper has appeared during your browser session.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies and identifiers support usage measurement. Their duration depends on Google's defaults and the applicable configuration.
  • Forum features, when available: drafts can be saved in local storage, and a sign-in token can be held in session storage. Clearing site data removes those browser copies and may sign you out.

You can manage cookies and site storage in your browser settings. Blocking cookies alone may not stop every analytics request. Google provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers. Browser storage controls do not delete messages you have already sent through email or another service.

The portfolio helper and community forum

The portfolio helper matches questions to prepared answers in your browser. It does not send the conversation text to an AI provider. Following one of its email or WhatsApp links opens that separate service.

Hosting, fonts and external services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves requests from your browser to Google's servers and exposes connection information such as your IP address. Google's services are covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Social sharing, video and other external links take you to services such as LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X and YouTube. A sharing link can pass the article's address and title to your chosen platform. Those services have their own privacy practices. Providers may process information in countries outside India.

Retention and protection

Enquiry correspondence may be retained while it is needed to respond, manage an ongoing engagement, maintain relevant business records or address a dispute or legal obligation. The appropriate period depends on the purpose and record. Contact me if you need information about a particular enquiry.

Analytics retention depends on the Google Analytics settings and Google's processing policies. Hosting and communication providers control retention of their own service records. Cookie expiry is separate from the retention of information already sent to a provider.

The website uses HTTPS to protect traffic in transit. No website, browser storage or communication service can guarantee complete security, so share only the information needed for your enquiry.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can read the portfolio and blog without sending a contact enquiry. You can also manage browser storage, use the Analytics opt-out tool, and choose whether to follow external links.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or raise a privacy concern. Email sajjalbajaj@gmail.com and describe your request. I may need proportionate information to verify that the request concerns your own records. Some records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations.

If you believe a child has shared personal information through a contact channel, please contact me so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this notice

This notice may change when the website's features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes to collection or use will be explained here.
