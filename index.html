01 Who I help

Most of my work is with small and mid-sized businesses that make, store, move or resell physical goods.

Manufacturers

Bills of materials, work centres, manufacturing orders and the stock and costing that depend on them.

Distributors and traders

Stock accuracy across warehouses, replenishment, purchasing and a clean path from order to delivery to invoice.

Teams leaving spreadsheets

Businesses replacing spreadsheets or older tools with one system, who need the move planned and the data cleaned first.

Stalled Odoo projects

Odoo databases that went live but don't match how the business works, or projects stuck between scope and go-live.

Problems I'm usually asked to fix

  • Stock figures in the system don't match the shelves, so nobody trusts them.
  • Buying is done by gut feel, leading to stock-outs on some items and overstock on others.
  • Production plans, component availability and costs live in separate spreadsheets.
  • Sales, warehouse and accounts each re-type the same order.
  • Reports take days to assemble and are out of date when they arrive.

02 About me

I started as a web developer in July 2016, building client websites and applications at ITpark and then Techmind Softwares. In November 2020 I joined Master Software Solutions (MSS) as a senior developer and team lead, and moved step by step towards the business side of projects: Scrum Master from 2022 to 2024, business analyst and project manager since 2022, and ERP consultant since October 2024, where my focus is Odoo.

On an Odoo project, my own contribution is the part between the business and the software. I run the requirement workshops, map current and future processes, decide what standard Odoo can do and where a gap is real, configure the standard apps, and write the specifications when something has to be built. I plan the project, prepare test scenarios with the people who will use the system, plan the data migration, train users and support the first weeks after go-live.

MSS is an Odoo implementation partner, and on larger projects I work alongside its developers and consultants. When I describe a project here, I describe my part of it; products such as MSS's route-planning module belong to MSS. The evidence behind my experience is on this site: the client projects listed on my CV, the certifications I've earned, the presentation I gave at Odoo Experience India 2026, and the Odoo guides I write.

Outside Odoo, I bring Power BI reporting, experience on Microsoft Dynamics 365 projects, a developer's understanding of how software gets built, and Agile delivery habits from my Scrum Master years.
