## What I'd tell a similar business

- If one person holds all the knowledge of your system, that's a risk on your balance sheet. Get it documented while you can.
- Running accounting in a separate system means reconciling forever. Moving it is a one-off effort.
- Route planning only works if delivery orders are complete and addresses are accurate. Check the data before you judge the tool.
