## What I'd tell a similar business

- If quality data is typed in by hand, you have a recall risk, not just an admin task. Capture readings from the equipment where you can.
- Plan for every market you intend to enter before you configure the first one.
- Flexible delivery windows are a sales feature. Customers notice when the system bends to their schedule.
