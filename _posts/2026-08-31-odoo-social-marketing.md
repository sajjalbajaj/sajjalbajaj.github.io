Keeping up with social media across several platforms, posting, scheduling, watching for responses, is a
scattered, time-consuming job. Odoo Social Marketing brings it into one place. Continuing the series on Odoo
Marketing, here is social.

## One place for your channels

Instead of logging into each platform separately, Social Marketing lets you manage your connected social
channels from a single dashboard in Odoo. Posting and monitoring happen in one place, which saves time and
gives you a clearer overview of your social presence.

## Post and schedule ahead

Consistency matters on social media, and it is hard to keep up in the moment. Odoo lets you create posts and
schedule them ahead, so you can plan a run of content and let it publish on time rather than scrambling
daily. Planned, consistent posting beats sporadic bursts.

## Monitor and engage

Social media is a two-way street. Being able to see activity and engagement in one place helps you keep on
top of your channels and respond, rather than missing things across scattered apps.

## See what performs

Odoo gives you visibility of how your social content performs, so you can learn what your audience responds
to and do more of it. As with the rest of Odoo Marketing, the point is to move from guessing to knowing.

## Part of your wider marketing

Because it lives in Odoo alongside email, SMS and the rest, social is part of one joined-up marketing
picture rather than a silo. Your channels work together toward the same goals, managed and measured in one
system.

## Practical tips

- Manage and monitor your channels from one place to save time.
- Plan and schedule content ahead for consistency.
- Watch what performs and lean into what your audience engages with.
- Keep social aligned with your other marketing, not separate from it.

## My take

Social Marketing will not replace a genuine social strategy, but it removes the scattered, time-consuming
mechanics of running several channels. Managing, scheduling and measuring from one place, alongside the rest
of your marketing, lets you be consistent without it eating your day.
