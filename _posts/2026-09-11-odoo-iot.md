A lot of business still happens in the physical world: weighing, measuring, scanning, printing. Odoo IoT
connects that hardware to your system, so the real world talks to Odoo directly. Continuing this run through
more Odoo apps, here is IoT.

## Bridging hardware and software

IoT, the Internet of Things, is about connecting physical devices to your software. Odoo IoT lets you link
hardware, scales, barcode scanners, receipt and label printers, measurement tools, cameras, to Odoo, so data
flows straight from the device into the system instead of being read off and typed in.

## Why it matters

Every time someone reads a number off a device and types it into a computer, there is delay and a chance of
error. Connecting the device removes that step: the weight, the scan, the measurement goes straight into
Odoo. Faster and more accurate, with less manual effort.

## Common uses

The practical uses are everywhere physical work meets data. A scale in shipping weighing parcels. Scanners in
the warehouse. Printers producing labels and receipts at the point of sale or dispatch. Measurement tools in
quality control feeding readings directly into a quality check. Each connection removes a manual step.

## Especially useful in operations

IoT shines in manufacturing, warehousing, quality and point of sale, anywhere hardware is part of the daily
flow. Connecting those devices makes operations smoother and the data more reliable, because the system is fed
by the equipment rather than by someone re-keying.

## Part of the connected picture

The theme of the whole Odoo story is connection, and IoT extends it to the physical world. Your devices become
part of the same system as your sales, stock and accounts, so what happens on the floor is reflected in the
software instantly.

## Practical tips

- Look for the points where people read a device and type the number in.
- Connect high-volume or error-prone devices first for the biggest gain.
- Use IoT in quality and dispatch to capture readings and weights directly.
- Treat connected devices as part of your one system, not add-ons.

## My take

IoT closes the gap between the physical work of a business and its software. By feeding weights, scans,
measurements and prints straight into Odoo, it removes manual steps and the errors they bring. For operations
where hardware is part of the daily flow, that direct connection is a quiet but real efficiency.
