Continuing the series on business problems and how Odoo solves them, here is one that hits revenue
directly: leads and customers slipping through the cracks.

## The problem

A promising enquiry lands in someone's inbox and quietly gets forgotten. A follow-up that should have
happened never does. Nobody is quite sure which deals are live or what the next step is. And existing
customers drift away because no one stayed in touch.

The tell-tale signs:

- Leads live in inboxes, sticky notes and people's heads, not one shared place.
- Follow-ups depend on memory, so some simply never happen.
- No one can see the pipeline: what is in play, at what stage, worth how much.
- When a salesperson is away, their deals go quiet.

Every one of those is lost revenue you already paid to attract.

## Why it hurts

Winning a lead is expensive. You spend on marketing, on time, on effort to create interest. To let that
interest die from a missed follow-up is money poured away. And keeping an existing customer is far
cheaper than finding a new one, so letting them drift is doubly costly. Disorganised selling is one of
the most expensive habits a business can have.

## How Odoo helps

Odoo CRM is built to make sure nothing slips:

- **One pipeline** shows every opportunity, its stage and its value, on a single visual board.
- **Scheduled activities** put the next follow-up, call or email on the deal itself, so it never relies
  on memory.
- **Full history** keeps every call, email and note about a customer in one place, so anyone can pick up
  a deal with full context.
- **Automatic capture** turns website enquiries and emails into leads, so nothing lands and disappears.

The effect is simple but powerful: every lead is captured, every deal has a next step, and no customer
is forgotten.

## Where to start

Get your leads and open deals into the pipeline first, and make scheduling a next activity on every
opportunity the one habit your team always follows. That single discipline, always having a next step,
wins more deals than any clever feature.

## My take

Losing leads and customers is rarely a selling-skill problem. It is an organisation problem, and
organisation is exactly what a CRM provides. Odoo CRM makes sure the interest you worked hard to create
actually gets followed up and turned into revenue. For most businesses, that is money currently left on
the table.

If deals and customers are slipping away, that is a fixable problem, and a common one. Happy to help you
turn it around.
