For a services business, billing is where delivery becomes revenue, and it is often where money leaks:
hours forgotten, work under-billed, invoices delayed. Odoo connects the work to the invoice so nothing is
lost. Continuing the series on Odoo Services and Project, here is billing project work.

## The problem with manual billing

When time is logged in one place, work managed in another, and invoices raised from memory in a third,
things slip. Billable hours get forgotten, invoices go out late, and clients query amounts nobody can quite
justify. Every gap between doing the work and billing it is money at risk.

## Time flows into invoices

Because Odoo connects Timesheets, Project and invoicing, the hours your team logs against a project can flow
straight into an invoice. You bill for the work that was actually recorded, accurately, rather than
reconstructing it. What was done gets billed.

## Different billing models

Services are billed in different ways, by time, at a fixed price, on milestones, and Odoo supports these
approaches so you can bill the way each project is agreed. The invoicing policy on the work determines what
becomes billable, so billing matches the deal.

## Accurate, prompt, defensible

Because invoices are built from recorded work, they are accurate and easy to justify to a client. And
because the data is already there, you can bill promptly rather than letting it pile up. Accurate and prompt
billing is one of the simplest ways to improve cash flow.

## One connected flow

The whole point is connection: plan the work, do it, log the time, bill it, and see it in your accounts,
all in one system. No re-keying between tools, no gaps for revenue to leak through. Delivery and billing are
two ends of the same thread.

## Practical tips

- Log time reliably, it is the basis for accurate billing.
- Set the right billing model and invoicing policy per project.
- Bill promptly from recorded work rather than reconstructing later.
- Use the connected data to justify invoices and speed up payment.

## My take

Billing is where a services business either captures the value it created or quietly loses some of it. By
connecting timesheets and projects to invoicing, Odoo makes sure the work you do is the work you bill,
accurately and promptly. Close that gap and both your revenue and your cash flow improve.
