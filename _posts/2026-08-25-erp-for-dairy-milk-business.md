Few industries move as fast or spoil as quickly as dairy. Milk and its products are perishable,
traceability is essential, and the business runs from farmer collection all the way to daily delivery.
Continuing the series on industry problems and how ERP solves them, here is dairy.

## Problem: perishability and short shelf life

Dairy spoils, so selling the oldest stock first is not optional, it is the difference between profit and
waste.

**How Odoo solves it:** lot and expiry tracking with first-expiry-first-out removal ensures the stock
closest to expiry ships first, cutting waste and protecting quality.

## Problem: traceability is essential

For food safety and standards, you must be able to trace a product back to its batch and source. Without
it, a recall or quality query is a nightmare.

**How Odoo solves it:** batch and lot tracking records each product's origin and follows it through
processing and delivery, so full traceability is built in, not bolted on.

## Problem: procurement from many farmers

Collecting milk from many suppliers, often with quantities and quality varying daily, is complex to
record and pay for fairly.

**How Odoo solves it:** purchasing in one system captures what comes in from each supplier and keeps the
records straight, so procurement and payments stay accurate.

## Problem: daily route distribution

Dairy is delivered constantly, on routes, to many customers, with orders that change every day. Managing
that on paper is relentless.

**How Odoo solves it:** orders, deliveries and customers together give a clear, organised view of daily
distribution, so routes run smoothly and deliveries are accurate.

## Problem: quality and pricing vary

Milk pricing and processing can depend on quality measures like fat content, and tracking that manually
is difficult.

**How Odoo solves it:** with quality checks and the right product setup, you can capture the measures
that matter and keep pricing and processing consistent.
