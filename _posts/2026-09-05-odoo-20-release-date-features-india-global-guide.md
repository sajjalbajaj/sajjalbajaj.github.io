**Short answer:** Odoo 20 is released, and the useful next step depends on your question. Check release and hosting facts, assess a specific capability, validate Indian localisation or plan an upgrade. This guide directs you to those separate decisions instead of repeating an outdated launch preview.

## Which official references establish the basics?

Odoo's [support table](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/20.0/administration/standard_extended_support.html) records release and support status. The [release notes](https://www.odoo.com/odoo-20-release-notes) identify product changes, while detailed documentation explains individual workflows.

None of those references proves that a particular business's custom code, external services or local processes have been accepted. Keep product availability separate from implementation evidence.

This article originally carried a September 2026 preview title. Its content and title have been revised after release, with the original address retained so existing links continue to work.

## How should a reader move from a feature to a test?

Choose one outcome and follow the detailed guide. For example, a manufacturing team interested in overlapping operations should read [continuous production](/blog/2026/11/12/odoo-20-continuous-production/), rather than infer a production design from a general AI or release summary.

A finance reviewer can start with [payment statuses](/blog/2026/11/10/odoo-20-bank-consistency-payment-status/). A team considering action taking assistants should first understand [AI agent boundaries](/blog/2026/11/04/odoo-20-ai-agents-governance/).

These narrower questions lead to clearer acceptance conditions and reduce the temptation to label every change an automatic business benefit.

## What information helps an implementation discussion?

Bring the current version, edition, hosting, important applications and a list of custom modules or integrations. Describe the present problem and the result the next team needs to rely on.

For an illustrative enquiry, “we need the first partial output to reach testing sooner” is more useful than “we want every Odoo 20 feature”. The former identifies a process and a candidate test; the latter leaves the scope undefined.

## What should you expect from these articles?

The guides distinguish documented behaviour from recommendations and proposed scenarios. They do not report customer test results unless explicitly supported, and they do not certify accounting, payroll or other regulated outcomes.

The [editorial approach](/editorial-policy/) explains source reviews and corrections. Use the [ERP acceptance evidence worksheet](/resources/erp-acceptance-evidence/) to record expected and observed results, then return to [all Odoo guides](/odoo/) for the relevant application topic.
