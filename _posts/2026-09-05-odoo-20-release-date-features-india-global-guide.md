Odoo 20 is coming, and if you run your business on Odoo or are thinking about it, this is the release to watch. Here is
a complete, plain-English preview in one place: when it launches, when you can actually use it, what is new, what is
getting better, and what it all means for businesses globally and in India.

A quick note on accuracy: as of early September 2026, Odoo 20 has not launched yet. Everything below is based on Odoo's
public roadmap and what has been shared ahead of the launch. Odoo only confirms the complete, final feature set at the
launch itself, so treat this as an informed preview, not a finished spec.

## When does Odoo 20 launch?

Odoo 20 is launched at Odoo Experience 2026, held from 24 to 26 September 2026 at Brussels Expo in Belgium. The launch
keynote, delivered by Odoo's founder, is where the new version is officially unveiled. So the launch date to mark is
late September 2026.

## When can you actually use it?

This is the important distinction: launch day is not the same as production-ready. The stable, production-ready version
usually arrives a few weeks after the conference, with the first solid patch typically landing around October or
November 2026. Experienced partners generally recommend upgrading a little after that first stable patch rather than on
day one, so the sensible planning window is late 2026 into 2027.

## Global and India availability

Odoo releases its new versions globally at the same time. There is no separate, delayed India release: when Odoo 20 is
out, it is out for everyone, including Indian businesses, on Odoo Online, Odoo.sh and on-premise. For India specifically,
the localization that Indian businesses rely on, GST, e-invoicing, e-way bills, GSTR reporting, TDS and TCS, and Indian
payroll with PF, ESI and professional tax, carries forward into Odoo 20 and continues to be maintained and improved.

## The headline: from AI helper to AI agent

If Odoo 19 was about more features and polish, Odoo 20's big theme is intelligence and simplification. The headline is
the move toward agentic AI: where earlier versions used AI as a helpful assistant that answered questions and drafted
content, Odoo 20 is expected to push toward AI that can carry out tasks on its own inside your workflows. That shift,
from an AI that helps you work to an AI that does some of the work, is the story of this release.

## What is new, and what is getting upgraded

Beyond AI, the expected new arrivals include a redesigned mobile app, new industry-specific solutions (for example
hospitality, construction and more), and technical additions aimed at speed and scale. On top of that, existing areas
are set to be upgraded: simpler payroll, enhanced accounting, improved CRM, smarter marketing automation and better
inventory. I cover the genuinely new features and the upgrades to existing ones in more detail in separate posts.

## What this means for you

The practical takeaways are simple. If you are on an older version, Odoo 20 is a good reason to plan an upgrade, but do it
after the first stable patch, not on launch day. If you are choosing an ERP now, it is worth knowing this direction of
travel. And if you are in India, you get the same release as everyone else, with the local compliance you need carried
through.

## My take

Odoo 20 looks like a meaningful release, less about piling on features and more about making the system smarter and
simpler, with agentic AI as the flagship. The best move for most businesses is not to rush, but to understand what is
coming, plan the upgrade for after the first stable patch, and get expert help to do it cleanly. I will be following the
launch closely and sharing what is actually confirmed.
