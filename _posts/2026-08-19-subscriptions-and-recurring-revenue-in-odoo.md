Recurring revenue is one of the most valuable things a business can build: predictable income you can plan
around, rather than starting each month from zero. If you sell anything on a repeating basis, Odoo
Subscriptions is built to manage it. Here is what it does.

## What subscriptions handle

A subscription is a sale that repeats on a schedule: monthly, quarterly, yearly. Instead of raising a new
order every cycle, you set up the subscription once, and Odoo takes care of the repetition, the invoicing
and the renewals. Think memberships, retainers, support plans, or any product or service billed regularly.

## Automatic recurring invoicing

The core benefit is automation. Once a subscription is running, Odoo generates the invoices on schedule,
so you are not manually raising the same bill every month. This removes a tedious, error-prone task and
makes sure income is billed on time, every time. Combined with online payment, much of the cycle can run
with very little manual effort.

## Renewals and changes

Subscriptions are not static, and Odoo handles their lifecycle:

- **Renewals:** subscriptions can continue or be renewed at the end of a term, keeping the revenue flowing.
- **Upsells and changes:** customers can move up or down a plan, add products, or change quantities, and
  the billing adjusts.
- **Endings:** subscriptions can be closed when a customer leaves, so you stop billing cleanly.

Managing this in one place means you always know who is subscribed to what, and what it is worth.

## The metrics that matter

Because subscriptions are about recurring value, Odoo helps you see the numbers that matter for this kind
of business, like your recurring revenue and how it is trending. That visibility turns a collection of
individual subscriptions into a clear picture of the health and growth of your recurring income.

## Why it is worth it

Recurring revenue changes how a business feels. Instead of chasing new sales every month just to stand
still, you build a base of predictable income and grow from there. Automating the billing and renewals
removes the admin that would otherwise make subscriptions a burden.

## Practical tips

- Set clear recurring plans and periods that match how you actually want to bill.
- Lean on automatic invoicing and online payment to keep the cycle low-effort.
- Watch your recurring revenue and churn, not just one-off sales.
- Handle upsells and cancellations promptly so billing always reflects reality.

## My take

If any part of your business can be sold on a repeating basis, subscriptions are worth serious thought.
Odoo automates the invoicing and renewals and shows you the recurring numbers that matter, turning
predictable revenue from an admin headache into a genuine strength.
