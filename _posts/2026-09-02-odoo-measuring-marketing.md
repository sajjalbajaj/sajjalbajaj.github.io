Marketing without measurement is just spending and hoping. But measuring the wrong things is almost as bad
as measuring nothing. To close the series on Odoo Marketing, here is how to measure what actually matters.

## Beyond vanity metrics

Opens, clicks, likes and followers are easy to count and easy to feel good about, but on their own they are
vanity metrics. They tell you something happened, not whether it helped the business. Real measurement
connects marketing activity to outcomes: leads, customers and revenue.

## Odoo measures the whole journey

Because marketing, CRM and sales live in one Odoo system, you can follow the whole journey: a campaign
generated this many leads, that many became opportunities, and that many became paying customers. That
end-to-end view is what turns marketing from a cost centre into something you can invest in with confidence.

## The metrics worth watching

A few that genuinely matter:

- **Leads generated:** is your marketing filling the pipeline?
- **Conversion to opportunities and customers:** is that interest turning into real business?
- **Cost of acquiring a customer:** what does it actually cost to win one, and is it sustainable?
- **Which channels and campaigns convert:** where should you put more effort, and less?

These tie effort to results, which is the whole point.

## Dashboards bring it together

Odoo's reporting, and tools like Power BI on top of it, let you bring these numbers together into clear
dashboards. Instead of pulling figures from separate marketing tools and guessing how they connect, you see
the picture in one place, from spend to revenue.

## Measure, then act

The point of measurement is decisions. See which campaigns and channels produce customers, do more of those,
and cut what only produces clicks. Marketing improves when it is guided by honest numbers rather than by
what feels busy.

## Practical tips

- Look past opens and clicks to leads, customers and revenue.
- Track the cost of acquiring a customer, not just activity.
- Use dashboards to see the whole journey in one place.
- Act on the numbers: invest in what converts, drop what does not.

## My take

Measuring marketing well is about connecting activity to outcomes, and that is exactly where Odoo's one-system
approach pays off: campaign to lead to customer to revenue, all visible. Watch the metrics that tie to real
business results, and let them guide where your marketing effort and money go.
