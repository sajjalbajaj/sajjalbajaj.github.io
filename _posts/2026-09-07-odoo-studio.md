Every business is a little different, and standard software rarely fits perfectly. Odoo Studio lets you
tailor Odoo to how you actually work, often without a developer. Continuing the series on Odoo Productivity,
here is Studio.

## Customize without code

Studio is a visual tool for adjusting Odoo to your needs: adding fields, changing screens, tweaking views and
even building simple custom apps, using a point-and-click interface rather than programming. It puts a good
deal of customization within reach of non-developers.

## Common, practical changes

The everyday value is in the small, useful changes: a field you need that is not there by default, a screen
laid out the way your team thinks, a report tweaked to your format. Studio makes these adjustments quick, so
the system fits your process instead of forcing your process to fit the system.

## Build simple apps

Beyond tweaks, Studio can help build simple custom applications for needs that no standard module covers.
For straightforward requirements, that can mean a tailored solution without a full development project.

## Where the line is

Studio is powerful, but it is not a replacement for proper development on complex requirements. The skill is
knowing when a Studio change is the right, quick answer, and when a need is complex enough to deserve proper
custom development, kept disciplined and upgrade-friendly. Used well, Studio handles the many small things so
development is reserved for the genuinely hard ones.

## Keep it disciplined

As with any customization, restraint matters. A tidy set of thoughtful changes keeps your system clean and
upgrade-friendly; a sprawl of ad-hoc tweaks does the opposite. Change with purpose, and document what you do.

## Practical tips

- Use Studio for the small, practical changes that make Odoo fit your team.
- Add only the fields and screens you genuinely need, avoid clutter.
- Know when a need is complex enough for proper development instead.
- Keep customization disciplined and documented, so upgrades stay smooth.

## My take

Studio is what lets Odoo bend to your business without a developer for every change. Used with discipline, it
handles the many small adjustments that make software actually fit, while leaving genuinely complex work to
proper development. That balance, tailored but tidy, is exactly what keeps an ERP both useful and
maintainable.
