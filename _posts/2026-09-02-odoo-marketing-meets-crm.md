Marketing and sales are often two separate worlds, and the gap between them is where leads get lost.
Marketing creates interest, then hands over a list, and things fall through the cracks. In Odoo, they live
in one system. Continuing the series on Odoo Marketing, here is where marketing meets CRM.

## The classic disconnect

In many businesses, marketing runs in one tool and sales in another. A campaign generates interest,
someone exports the responders, and sales gets a spreadsheet, if they get anything at all. Context is lost,
follow-up is slow, and good leads go cold. The handover is the weak point.

## One system closes the gap

Because Odoo Marketing and CRM are part of the same platform, the interest your campaigns create can flow
straight into your sales pipeline as leads and opportunities. There is no export and import, no lost
context, no gap for leads to fall into. Marketing feeds sales directly.

## Sales sees the full story

When a lead reaches sales in Odoo, it carries its history: which campaign brought them in, what they
engaged with, what they are interested in. Salespeople start the conversation informed, which makes
follow-up more relevant and more likely to convert.

## Feedback flows both ways

The connection is two-way. Sales outcomes, what converted, what did not, become visible to marketing, so you
can see which campaigns actually produced customers, not just clicks. That lets you invest in the marketing
that genuinely drives revenue.

## Measure the whole journey

Because it is all one system, you can see the whole journey from first touch to closed deal. That end-to-end
view is what lets you understand your real cost of acquiring a customer and which channels are truly worth
it, rather than judging marketing on vanity metrics.

## Practical tips

- Let campaign responses flow into your CRM as leads, not exported lists.
- Make sure sales can see where a lead came from and what they engaged with.
- Feed sales outcomes back to marketing to learn what really works.
- Judge marketing on pipeline and revenue, not just opens and clicks.

## My take

The biggest win in marketing is often not a better campaign, it is closing the gap between marketing and
sales. Odoo does that by keeping both in one system, so interest becomes pipeline without loss, and you can
finally see which marketing actually drives revenue. That connection is worth more than any single feature.
