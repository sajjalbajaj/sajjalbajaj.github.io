People love spreadsheets for a reason: they are flexible and familiar. The problem is that a spreadsheet of
business data is out of date the moment you export it. Odoo Spreadsheet keeps the flexibility but connects it
to live data. Continuing the series on Odoo Productivity, here is Spreadsheet.

## Familiar spreadsheet power

Spreadsheet gives you the spreadsheet experience you already know, formulas, tables, formatting, right inside
Odoo. So you keep the flexibility to slice and analyse data your own way, without learning a new tool.

## The key difference: live data

Here is what makes it more than a spreadsheet. It can pull live data from Odoo, so your figures reflect the
current state of the business. Instead of exporting to a static file that is stale immediately, your analysis
stays connected and up to date.

## Build reports that refresh themselves

Because the data is live, you can build reports and dashboards that refresh rather than being rebuilt every
period. The monthly numbers, the sales view, the stock summary, they update from the source, saving the
repetitive export-and-rebuild cycle that eats so much time.

## Analyse without breaking anything

Spreadsheet is a safe place to explore and model. You can work with your data, try scenarios and build views,
without touching the underlying records. It gives analysts and managers freedom without risk.

## Part of the bigger reporting picture

Spreadsheet complements Odoo's built-in reports and tools like Power BI. For flexible, ad-hoc analysis in a
familiar format, connected to live data, it fills a real gap between rigid reports and disconnected external
spreadsheets.

## Practical tips

- Use Spreadsheet for flexible, ad-hoc analysis on live data.
- Build recurring reports that refresh instead of rebuilding each time.
- Keep heavy, standardised reporting in dedicated tools, and use Spreadsheet for exploration.
- Remember the data is live, so your analysis stays current.

## My take

Spreadsheet gives you the best of both worlds: the flexibility and familiarity of a spreadsheet, with data
that stays live because it comes from Odoo. That kills the biggest weakness of the exported spreadsheet, going
stale instantly, and saves the endless rebuild cycle. For anyone who lives in spreadsheets, it is a genuine
upgrade.
