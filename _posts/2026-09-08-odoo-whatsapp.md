For a huge number of customers, WhatsApp is simply how they communicate. Meeting them there, from inside
your business system, is powerful. Odoo integrates WhatsApp so you can. Continuing the series on Odoo
Productivity, here is WhatsApp.

## Reach customers where they are

The strength of WhatsApp is reach and familiarity: customers already use it and read it. Odoo's WhatsApp
integration lets you communicate with customers on that channel directly from your system, rather than
asking them to come to yours.

## Useful, timely messages

WhatsApp works especially well for timely, transactional messages: order confirmations, updates,
reminders and quick replies to questions. These are the messages customers actually want, and delivering
them on WhatsApp means they are seen promptly.

## Connected to your data

Because it is part of Odoo, WhatsApp messaging draws on your real customer data and connects to the records
it relates to. A message about an order can sit with that order; a conversation with a customer is part of
their record. It is business messaging in context, not a separate phone.

## Respect the channel

WhatsApp is personal, so it must be used with care and consent. The businesses that benefit send messages
that are relevant, expected and helpful, not marketing spam. Respecting the channel keeps customers engaged
rather than blocking you.

## Part of your communication mix

WhatsApp joins email, SMS and the rest as another way to reach people, chosen for when it fits best. Having
it inside Odoo means it is part of one connected communication picture, using the same data and sitting with
the same records.

## Practical tips

- Use WhatsApp for timely, useful messages customers welcome.
- Always respect consent and keep messages relevant.
- Let conversations connect to the customer and order records.
- Choose the right channel for each message: WhatsApp, SMS or email.

## My take

WhatsApp integration meets customers on the channel they already live in, for the timely, transactional
messages that genuinely help. Kept respectful and connected to your Odoo data, it becomes a natural part of
how you communicate, rather than another disconnected inbox. For many businesses, that reach is significant.
