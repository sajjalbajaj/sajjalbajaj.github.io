Every business drowns a little in files: contracts, invoices, scans, forms, spread across drives, inboxes
and desks. Odoo Documents brings order and even automation to all of it. Continuing the series on Odoo
Productivity, here is Documents.

## One place for your files

Documents gives you a central, organised place to store and manage files, with folders and structure, so
paperwork is findable rather than scattered. Instead of "which drive was that on", you have one organised
home for your documents.

## Organise and share

You can organise files sensibly and control who sees what, so the right people have access to the right
documents and sensitive material stays protected. Sharing is cleaner than emailing attachments around and
losing track of versions.

## Automate the boring handling

Here is where Documents goes beyond storage. It can automate routine document handling, routing files to the
right place, requesting validation, or turning a document into an action. That takes a chunk of tedious
filing and processing off people's plates.

## Connected to the business

Because Documents is part of Odoo, files connect to the records they relate to, and can feed into workflows
like accounting. A vendor bill document, for example, is not just a file, it can flow into the process it
belongs to. Paperwork becomes part of the work, not a separate pile.

## Less clutter, less risk

Organised, controlled documents mean less time lost searching, less risk of the wrong version or a lost
file, and easier compliance when you need to find something. Tidy files are quietly worth a lot.

## Practical tips

- Give your files one organised home rather than scattered drives.
- Control access so the right people see the right documents.
- Automate routine filing and validation where you can.
- Connect documents to the records and processes they belong to.

## My take

Documents turns file chaos into an organised, partly automated system that connects to the rest of your
business. Less searching, less version confusion, less manual filing. It is not glamorous, but for a
business buried in paperwork, getting documents under control is a real relief.
