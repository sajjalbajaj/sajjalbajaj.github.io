You can write the perfect email, but if it goes to the wrong people it still fails. In marketing, the
audience often matters more than the message. Continuing the series on Odoo Marketing, here is building and
segmenting your audience.

## Your data is your audience

The foundation of good marketing is a clean, well-organised contact database, and in Odoo that is not a
separate marketing list, it is the same customer data your whole business uses. Every contact, customer and
lead is already there, which means your marketing starts from real, current information rather than a stale
exported list.

## Why segmentation matters

Sending everyone the same message wastes the opportunity. A new lead, a loyal customer and a lapsed buyer
need different messages. Segmentation, dividing your audience into meaningful groups, lets you send each
group something relevant, which lifts results and reduces the annoyance that makes people unsubscribe.

## Segment by what you know

Because Odoo holds rich data about your contacts, you can segment on what actually matters: their type,
location, what they have bought, how they have engaged, where they are in your pipeline. The more relevant
the grouping, the more relevant the message you can send.

## One audience across every channel

The same well-segmented audience powers email, SMS, automation and events. You build and maintain your
understanding of your contacts once, and use it everywhere, rather than rebuilding lists per tool. That
consistency is a quiet but real advantage of marketing inside your ERP.

## Keep your data clean

Good targeting depends on good data. Keeping contact information accurate and up to date, and respecting
consent and preferences, is what keeps segmentation useful and your marketing trusted. Clean data in means
relevant marketing out.

## Practical tips

- Treat your Odoo contact data as your marketing foundation, and keep it clean.
- Segment by meaningful attributes, not just send to everyone.
- Match the message to the segment, relevance drives results.
- Respect consent and preferences to protect trust and deliverability.

## My take

The unglamorous truth of marketing is that audience and segmentation matter more than any single clever
campaign. Odoo's advantage is that your audience is your real, connected business data, ready to segment and
use across every channel. Get this right and every campaign you run works harder.
