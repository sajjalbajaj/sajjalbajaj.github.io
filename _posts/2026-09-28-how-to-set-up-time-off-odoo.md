Managing leave with emails and spreadsheets is error-prone and time-consuming. Odoo Time Off handles requests,
approvals and balances in one place, but it needs setting up to match your real policies. Continuing this series of
practical Odoo how-to guides, here is time off.

## Set up your leave types

Start by defining the types of leave your business offers: annual leave, sick leave, unpaid leave and any others. Each
type can behave differently in terms of allowances and whether it is paid. Setting these up to match your actual
policies is the foundation, because everything else flows from how leave types are configured.

## Configure allocations

For leave that comes with an entitlement, such as a number of annual leave days, set up allocations so each employee has
the right balance. Odoo then tracks how much is used and how much remains. Getting allocations right means balances are
accurate and disputes about remaining days simply do not arise.

## Set up the approval flow

Decide who approves whose leave and set up the approval flow accordingly. Usually a manager approves their team's
requests. A clear approval path means requests are reviewed properly and employees know their leave is confirmed, rather
than wondering whether an email was seen. It removes a common source of uncertainty.

## Let employees request their own leave

The big time saver is self-service: employees request leave themselves, see their balances, and get a clear answer.
Setting this up takes the administrative burden off HR and managers and gives everyone visibility. It turns leave from a
paperwork exercise into a quick, clear process people can handle themselves.

## Connect to the calendar and planning

Approved time off should be visible where it matters: on calendars and in planning, so people know who is away. Setting
up this connection means leave is not a surprise that derails a project or leaves a shift uncovered. It keeps the whole
team's availability accurate and visible.

## Practical tips

- Define leave types that match your real policies.
- Set up allocations so entitlements and balances are accurate.
- Configure a clear approval flow, usually manager-based.
- Enable self-service so employees request and track their own leave.
- Connect time off to calendars and planning for visible availability.

## My take

Time off is a small process that causes real friction when handled manually, and Odoo Time Off removes almost all of it.
Leave types that match your policies, correct allocations, a clear approval flow and employee self-service turn leave
management into something quick and transparent. Connect it to planning and you also stop leave from catching projects
and shifts by surprise.
