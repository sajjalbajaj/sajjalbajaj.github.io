Most leads are not ready to buy the moment they arrive, and manually following up with each one over weeks
is impossible at scale. Marketing Automation solves that by nurturing leads for you. Continuing the series
on Odoo Marketing, here is automation.

## What marketing automation does

Marketing automation lets you build campaigns that run themselves based on what people do. Someone signs up,
so they receive a welcome sequence. Someone clicks a link, so they get a relevant follow-up. Instead of you
manually sending each message, the campaign responds to behaviour automatically.

## Build flows, not one-off blasts

Rather than a single email to everyone, Odoo lets you design a flow: a series of steps with conditions and
timing. If this happens, do that; wait a few days, then send this. You map out the journey once, and Odoo
runs it for every contact who enters it.

## Nurture leads over time

The real value is nurturing. Few people buy on first contact, but a thoughtful sequence that stays in touch,
provides value and gently moves people forward turns more interest into action, without anyone chasing each
lead by hand. Your marketing works while you sleep.

## Powered by your data

Because it is part of Odoo, automation acts on your real contact and behaviour data. It can react to what
people do across your marketing and connect to your CRM, so the right people get the right nudges based on
where they actually are.

## Keep it helpful, not spammy

Automation makes it easy to send a lot, which is exactly why restraint matters. The best automated campaigns
feel helpful and well-timed, not relentless. Aim to be genuinely useful at each step, and people stay
engaged rather than unsubscribing.

## Practical tips

- Start with one clear journey, like welcoming and nurturing new leads.
- Base steps on real behaviour, so messages stay relevant.
- Space messages sensibly, helpful beats frequent.
- Measure the flow and refine the steps that underperform.

## My take

Marketing Automation turns nurturing from an impossible manual task into something that runs on autopilot,
responding to what people actually do. Built on your Odoo data and connected to your CRM, it quietly moves
leads toward becoming customers. Keep it helpful, and it becomes one of your hardest-working tools.
