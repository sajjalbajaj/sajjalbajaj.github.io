Across this part of the series we have looked at the pieces, project, timesheets, planning, helpdesk, field
service, appointments and billing. To close it, here is how they fit together to run a whole services
business on Odoo.

## From lead to delivery, in one system

A services business has a natural flow: win the work, plan it, deliver it, support the client, bill for it,
and learn whether it paid. The strength of Odoo is that all of this lives in one connected system, so the
flow is unbroken rather than stitched across separate tools.

## Winning the work

It starts before delivery, in CRM. A lead becomes an opportunity, then a won deal. Because CRM is part of the
same system, the work you win flows straight into delivery, with the context intact, no handover gap.

## Planning and delivering

Project turns the work into clear, owned tasks. Planning schedules who does what and when. Timesheets capture
the time it takes. Field Service handles anything on site. Together they mean work is organised, assigned and
tracked, rather than managed by memory and chase-ups.

## Supporting the client

Once delivered, Helpdesk keeps support organised, so client issues are tracked and resolved rather than
lost. Good support protects the relationship and the repeat business that services firms depend on.

## Billing and profit

Timesheets and project work flow into billing, so you invoice accurately for what you did. Then analytic
accounting shows whether each project actually made money. Delivery, billing and profitability are joined
up, which is how a services business stays healthy, not just busy.

## Why one system matters here

Services businesses live on time, relationships and thin margins, and those are exactly what get lost when
tools do not talk. One connected system means hours are billed, clients are supported, and profitability is
visible, without the leaks that separate tools create.

## Practical tips

- Let won deals in CRM flow straight into projects.
- Plan and assign work, and log time reliably against it.
- Keep support organised so client relationships stay strong.
- Bill from recorded work, and measure profit per project.

## My take

Run piece by piece, a services business leaks time, revenue and margin at every handover. Run on one
connected system, from winning work to understanding profit, and those leaks close. That end-to-end
connection is the real reason a services firm benefits from Odoo, and it is exactly the kind of setup I help
businesses put in place.
