The single biggest headache in online selling is keeping the shop and the stockroom in agreement. Sell
something you do not have, and you disappoint a customer. Hold stock you cannot see online, and you miss
sales. Odoo solves this by making the shop and the inventory one system. Continuing the series on Odoo
Website and eCommerce, here is why that matters.

## The problem with bolt-on webshops

When your online store is a separate platform from your inventory, the two must be kept in sync, usually by
imports, plugins or manual updates. Every sync is a chance for them to drift apart, which leads to
overselling, stale stock figures and constant firefighting. The bigger you grow, the worse it gets.

## One catalog, one stock

In Odoo, the products you sell online are the same products in your inventory. There is one catalog and one
stock figure. When an item sells, online or through any other channel, it comes off the same stock. The
shop always reflects what you actually have.

## No overselling

Because online sales draw on real, shared stock, you are far less likely to sell something that is not
there. That protects the customer experience and saves you the awkward cancellations and apologies that
overselling causes.

## Every channel in one place

Many businesses sell through more than one route: online, in a shop, wholesale. In Odoo, all of these feed
the same inventory and orders. You get one true picture of stock and sales across every channel, instead of
piecing it together from separate systems.

## Restocking and planning stay accurate

When your online sales are part of the same inventory, your reordering rules and planning see them too. So
purchasing and production react to real total demand, not just what happened offline. The whole operation
plans on complete information.

## Practical tips

- Sell from one shared catalog and stock, not a separate webshop database.
- Trust the shared stock figure across all channels to avoid overselling.
- Let online demand feed your reordering and planning like any other sales.
- Manage all channels in one place for a true view of stock and sales.

## My take

The reason to run your store in Odoo is not just convenience, it is that the shop and the inventory are the
same system. That connection quietly removes the overselling, drift and firefighting that separate systems
create. One catalog, one stock, every channel in agreement. For anyone selling online and elsewhere, that
is a genuine advantage.
