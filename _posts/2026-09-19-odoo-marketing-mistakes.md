Marketing in Odoo is powerful precisely because it sits on the same data as sales and contacts, so it can be targeted
and measured. The common mistakes almost all come from ignoring that advantage. Continuing this series on common Odoo
mistakes, here is Marketing.

## Blasting everyone the same message

The classic mistake is sending one message to your entire list regardless of who people are. It gets ignored and
annoys, and over time it damages your ability to reach anyone. Use segmentation, which Odoo makes easy on your contact
data, so the right people get relevant messages. Relevance is what makes marketing work.

## Not connecting marketing to sales

When marketing and CRM are disconnected, you cannot tell which campaigns actually generated business. Because Odoo links
them, you can follow a lead from a campaign through to a closed deal. Skipping that connection means marketing stays a
cost you cannot justify rather than an investment you can prove.

## Ignoring the results

Sending campaigns and never looking at what happened is remarkably common. Open rates, clicks, conversions, they tell
you what is working and what to stop. Marketing without measurement is guessing. The data is right there; the mistake is
not using it to get better each time.

## Neglecting list hygiene

Marketing to a stale, dirty list, dead addresses, duplicates, people who never engage, hurts your deliverability and
your numbers. Keeping the list clean and respecting unsubscribes is not just good manners; it is what keeps your
messages landing in inboxes at all. Quality of list beats size of list.

## Set-and-forget automation

Marketing automation is a great tool and a trap if you set it up once and never revisit it. Automated journeys drift out
of date as your business and audience change. Treat automation as something to monitor and refine, not a machine you
switch on and forget.

## Practical tips

- Segment your audience so messages are relevant, not mass blasts.
- Connect marketing to CRM so you can prove what generates business.
- Look at the results and let them shape the next campaign.
- Keep your lists clean and honour unsubscribes.
- Revisit automation regularly rather than setting it and forgetting.

## My take

Marketing on Odoo rewards using the connection to your data and punishes ignoring it. Segmentation, a link to sales,
attention to results and clean lists are what turn marketing from noise into something measurable and effective. The
tools are capable; the mistakes are almost all about not using the connected data that makes them worth having.
