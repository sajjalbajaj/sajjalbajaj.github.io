The first question everyone asks about a new Odoo version is simple: when can I get it? For Odoo 20, the answer has two
parts, the launch date and the production-ready date, and the difference between them matters. Here is the timeline in
plain English.

A quick note first: as of early September 2026, Odoo 20 has not launched. What follows is based on Odoo's public
announcements and roadmap ahead of the launch. The final details are confirmed at the event itself.

## The launch date: late September 2026

Odoo 20 is launched at Odoo Experience 2026, which runs from 24 to 26 September 2026 at Brussels Expo in Belgium. The
new version is unveiled in the launch keynote given by Odoo's founder. Odoo Experience is a huge event, drawing tens of
thousands of attendees from around the world, and the Odoo 20 launch is its centrepiece.

## Launch is not the same as production-ready

Here is the part that trips people up. The launch is the announcement; it is not the moment you should move your live
business onto the new version. The stable, production-ready release typically follows a few weeks later, with the first
solid patch usually arriving around October or November 2026. Early releases of any major version tend to have rough
edges that the first patches smooth out.

## When you should actually upgrade

For most businesses, the sensible approach is to wait for that first stable patch and then plan the upgrade a little
after it, often four to eight weeks later. That gives the version time to settle and gives you time to plan properly.
Upgrading on launch day is for the adventurous and the testers, not for a business that needs its ERP working reliably
every day.

## Global availability: everyone at once

Odoo releases new versions globally at the same time. There is no staggered rollout by country: when Odoo 20 is
available, it is available worldwide, across Odoo Online, Odoo.sh and on-premise deployments. Whatever region you are in,
the timeline above applies to you.

## How to use the time before you upgrade

The gap between now and a sensible upgrade is not dead time; it is planning time. Use it to review what you customised,
check which of your apps and integrations will need attention, and decide what you want to gain from the upgrade. A
little preparation now makes the eventual upgrade far smoother. If you want to know more about doing upgrades cleanly,
Odoo's own documentation and an experienced partner are your best guides.

## My take

The Odoo 20 timeline is straightforward once you separate the two dates: launched in late September 2026, genuinely
ready for production a few weeks later. The best advice is not to rush. Let the version stabilise, plan your upgrade
properly, and move when it is solid, not when it is merely new. That patience is what turns an upgrade from a risk into
a smooth step forward.
