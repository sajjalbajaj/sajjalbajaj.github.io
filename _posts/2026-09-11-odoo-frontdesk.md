The way visitors are greeted says something about a business, and a paper sign-in book at reception is both
dated and easily lost. Odoo Frontdesk offers a simple, modern alternative. To round off this run through more
Odoo apps, here is Frontdesk.

## Self-service check-in

Frontdesk lets visitors check themselves in, typically on a screen at reception, entering who they are and who
they are there to see. It is quick for the visitor and gives your business a tidy, professional first
impression, with no scrabbling for a pen and a book.

## Automatic host notifications

When a visitor checks in, the person they are there to see can be notified automatically, so hosts know their
guest has arrived without reception having to chase them down. That smooths the arrival experience and saves
the front desk a round of phone calls.

## A clear visitor record

Because check-ins are captured in the system, you have a clear record of who visited, when and who they saw.
That is useful for security, for knowing who is on site, and for any situation where you need an accurate log
rather than a half-filled paper book.

## A professional first impression

First impressions count, especially for clients and candidates. A smooth, modern check-in signals that the
business is organised and current. It is a small thing that quietly reflects well on you.

## Part of the same system

Like the other apps in this series, Frontdesk lives in Odoo, so it fits alongside the rest of your workplace
tools rather than being a separate gadget at the door. Visitor management becomes one more thing handled in
your connected system.

## Practical tips

- Let visitors check in themselves for a quick, professional arrival.
- Use automatic host notifications so people know their guests have arrived.
- Keep the visitor record for security and knowing who is on site.
- Treat it as part of your connected workplace, not a standalone device.

## My take

Frontdesk is a small, focused app that upgrades a surprisingly visible part of your business: how visitors are
received. Self-service check-in, automatic notifications and a clear record replace the dog-eared sign-in book
with something professional and useful. A minor app, but a nice touch that reflects an organised business.
