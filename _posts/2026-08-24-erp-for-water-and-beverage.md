Water and beverage businesses run on high volumes, thin margins and constant delivery, with returnable
containers and batch production adding their own twists. Continuing the series on industry problems and
how ERP solves them, here is water and beverage.

## Problem: batch production is hard to track

Beverages are made in batches, and each batch needs to be recorded for stock, cost and quality. Done on
paper, it is error-prone and slow.

**How Odoo solves it:** manufacturing orders capture each batch, consuming inputs and producing finished
stock automatically, so production, inventory and cost stay in step.

## Problem: returnable containers and deposits

Bottles, jars and crates go out and, hopefully, come back, often with deposits attached. Tracking that
by hand is a headache and money gets lost.

**How Odoo solves it:** with the right product and inventory setup, you can track returnable containers
and their movement, so you know what is out with customers and manage deposits sensibly.

## Problem: route delivery is chaotic

Delivering to many customers on regular routes, with orders changing constantly, is hard to coordinate
from spreadsheets.

**How Odoo solves it:** orders, deliveries and customers in one system give your delivery operation a
clear, organised view of what goes where, so routes run smoothly and nothing is missed.

## Problem: expiry and freshness

Many beverages carry expiry dates, and shipping older stock last leads to waste and unhappy customers.

**How Odoo solves it:** lot and expiry tracking with first-expiry-first-out removal makes sure the
oldest suitable stock ships first, reducing waste and returns.

## Problem: thin margins leave no room for waste

High volume and low margin mean small inefficiencies add up fast, in overstock, waste and manual
errors.

**How Odoo solves it:** accurate stock, batch costing and automation strip out the waste and errors that
quietly eat thin margins, so more of the volume turns into profit.
