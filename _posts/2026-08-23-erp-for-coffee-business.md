A coffee business looks simple from the outside and is anything but: sourcing, roasting, blending,
freshness and selling across several channels all at once. Continuing the series on industry problems
and how ERP solves them, here is coffee.

## Problem: no traceability from bean to bag

Customers increasingly want to know where their coffee came from, and quality control depends on it. If
you cannot trace a bag back to its green bean lot, you have a problem.

**How Odoo solves it:** lot tracking records the origin of each batch of beans and follows it through
roasting, blending and packing, so you can trace any bag back to its source.

## Problem: roasting yield and loss are not tracked

Roasting loses weight, and if that loss is not captured, your stock and costs drift away from reality.

**How Odoo solves it:** modelling roasting as a production step lets Odoo account for input green beans
and output roasted coffee accurately, so yield and cost reflect what really happens in the roaster.

## Problem: blends are hard to manage

A blend is a recipe of several beans in set proportions. Managed by hand, consistency and costing
suffer.

**How Odoo solves it:** bills of materials define each blend precisely, so producing it consumes the
right beans in the right amounts and the cost of every blend is clear.

## Problem: freshness and dates matter

Coffee is best fresh, and selling older stock while newer sits behind it is both wasteful and bad for
customers.

**How Odoo solves it:** with lot and date tracking and sensible removal strategies, older stock is used
first, so you ship fresh and reduce waste.

## Problem: you sell through many channels

Wholesale, retail, online and subscriptions each have their own rhythm, and juggling them separately is
chaos.

**How Odoo solves it:** one connected system handles wholesale orders, retail, e-commerce and recurring
subscriptions together, all drawing on the same stock and customers.
