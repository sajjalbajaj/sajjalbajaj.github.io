Knowing who is at work, and for how long, matters for planning, payroll and fairness. Done on paper or
memory, it is inaccurate and disputed. Odoo Attendances makes it simple and reliable. Continuing the series
on Odoo HR and People, here is attendances.

## Simple check in and out

At its core, Attendances lets employees check in and out, recording their working time. This can be done in
a way that suits your workplace, for example a kiosk where staff clock in, so capturing time is quick and
part of the daily routine rather than a chore.

## Accurate work-time records

Because check-ins are recorded in the system, you get accurate, timestamped records of hours worked. That
removes the guesswork and disputes of manual timesheets, and gives you a trustworthy basis for decisions
that depend on hours.

## Feeds planning and payroll

Attendance data is not an island. It connects to the wider HR picture, informing planning and, where
relevant, feeding into payroll. Hours captured once flow to where they are needed, rather than being
re-entered, which is the advantage of HR living in one system.

## Visibility for managers

Managers can see attendance across their team, who is in, patterns over time, without chasing people or
piecing it together. That visibility supports better staffing decisions and fairer management.

## Fair and transparent

A clear, consistent record of working time is fairer for everyone. Employees know their hours are captured
accurately, and managers have an objective basis rather than impressions. Good records prevent
disagreements before they start.

## Practical tips

- Set up attendance capture in a way that fits your workplace and is easy to use.
- Use the accurate hours data rather than relying on memory or manual sheets.
- Let attendance feed planning and payroll instead of re-keying it.
- Review patterns to make better staffing and management decisions.

## My take

Attendances turns a fuzzy, often-disputed area into clean data: who worked, when, for how long, captured
simply and used across HR. It is a straightforward app that brings fairness and accuracy to something most
businesses handle poorly.
