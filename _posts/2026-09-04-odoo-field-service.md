If your business sends people out to do work, installs, repairs, maintenance, visits, coordinating it is a
real challenge: the right person, at the right place, with the right information, and a clean record
afterwards. Odoo Field Service manages exactly that. Continuing the series on Odoo Services and Project,
here is field service.

## Plan and dispatch jobs

Field Service lets you schedule on-site jobs and assign them to the right technician. Instead of coordinating
by phone and hoping, you plan who goes where and when, and dispatch the work clearly. Everyone knows their
jobs for the day.

## The technician has what they need

A job carries its details: the customer, the location, what needs doing and any relevant history. So the
person arriving on site has the context to do the work, rather than turning up half-informed. That means
fewer wasted visits and better first-time fix rates.

## Log work on site

Technicians can record what they did, time spent and any parts or products used, from the field. That
captures an accurate record at the source, rather than reconstructing it later, which keeps both your data
and your billing honest.

## Straight to billing

Because the work, time and materials are logged in Odoo, they can flow into billing. You invoice for what
was actually done, accurately and promptly, without chasing paper job sheets. The loop from job to invoice
is clean.

## Connected to the whole business

Field Service ties into your customers, inventory and accounts. Parts used come from stock, the job links to
the customer record, and the cost and revenue land in the books. On-site work is part of the connected
business, not a separate paper trail.

## Practical tips

- Schedule and assign jobs clearly so technicians know their day.
- Give each job the details and history needed for a first-time fix.
- Log time and parts on site, while it is accurate.
- Let completed jobs flow into billing rather than chasing job sheets.

## My take

Field Service brings order to the genuinely hard problem of coordinating on-site work: planning, dispatch,
on-site logging and billing, all connected. Get the right person to the right job with the right
information, capture the work cleanly, and bill accurately. For any field-based business, that end-to-end
flow is a big step up from phones and paper.
