Sharing knowledge, training staff, onboarding customers, or teaching a skill, does not scale when it happens
one conversation at a time. Odoo eLearning lets you package it into courses. Continuing this run through more
Odoo apps, here is eLearning.

## Build online courses

eLearning lets you create structured online courses: lessons, videos, documents, quizzes and more, organised
into a learning path. Instead of explaining the same thing over and over, you build it once and let people
learn at their own pace.

## Many uses

The app is versatile. Use it to train and onboard employees, to educate customers on your products, to
certify partners, or even to sell courses as a product in their own right. One tool covers internal training
and external education alike.

## Learn at your own pace

Because courses are online and self-paced, learners take them when it suits them, and revisit material when
they need to. That is more flexible and often more effective than a one-off session everyone has to attend at
the same time, and it scales to any number of people.

## Track progress

eLearning lets you see who has completed what, so training is not a hopeful email but something you can
actually track. For onboarding, compliance or certification, knowing that people have genuinely completed the
material matters.

## Connected to your business

Because it is built on Odoo Website and part of the wider system, eLearning connects to your site, your
contacts and, if you sell courses, your sales and eCommerce. Training is not a separate platform to manage; it
is part of the same connected business.

## Practical tips

- Package repetitive training and onboarding into reusable courses.
- Mix formats, video, text, quizzes, to keep learners engaged.
- Track completion where it matters, for onboarding or compliance.
- Consider selling courses if your knowledge is valuable to others.

## My take

eLearning turns knowledge that lives in people's heads and repeated conversations into structured, self-paced
courses that scale. Whether for staff, customers or as a product, it lets you teach once and reach many, all
connected to the rest of your Odoo business. For any organisation that trains people, that leverage is real.
