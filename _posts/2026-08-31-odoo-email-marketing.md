Email is still one of the most cost-effective ways to reach customers, but only if it is done well and
based on good data. Odoo Email Marketing puts campaign tools right next to the customer data you already
hold. This starts a short series on Odoo Marketing.

## Design emails without code

Odoo gives you a drag-and-drop email designer, so you can build professional, on-brand emails without
touching code. Choose a layout, drop in your content, images and calls to action, and preview how it looks
before it goes out. Good-looking emails, made quickly.

## Built on the data you already have

The real advantage is the audience. Because Email Marketing sits inside Odoo, it draws on your existing
contacts and customer data. You are not exporting lists and importing them into a separate tool; you email
the people already in your system, targeted by what you know about them.

## Send to the right people

Rather than blasting everyone with the same message, you can target segments of your audience, so the right
message reaches the right people. Relevant emails get better results and annoy fewer recipients, which
protects your sender reputation as well as your relationships.

## Measure what happens

Sending is only half of it. Odoo tracks how campaigns perform, opens, clicks and more, so you can see what
resonates and what falls flat. That turns email from guesswork into something you improve over time.

## Connected to the rest of the business

Because it is part of your ERP, email marketing connects to sales and CRM. Interest generated by a campaign
can feed into your pipeline, so marketing and sales work from the same picture rather than in separate
worlds. We will come back to that later in the series.

## Practical tips

- Keep emails clear and on-brand, with one main call to action.
- Target segments rather than emailing everyone the same thing.
- Watch opens and clicks, and learn from what works.
- Keep your contact data clean, good targeting depends on it.

## My take

Email Marketing in Odoo is powerful precisely because it is not a standalone tool: it uses the customer
data you already have, targets the right people, and connects results back to sales. Design well, target
thoughtfully, measure honestly, and email becomes a reliable channel rather than a shot in the dark.
