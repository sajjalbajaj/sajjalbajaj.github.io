A pipeline is only as good as what goes into it. If new leads are not flowing in, even the best sales
process runs dry. Odoo CRM gives you several ways to capture leads and even to go looking for new ones.
Here is how lead generation and lead mining work.

## Capturing leads automatically

The best lead capture happens without anyone typing. Odoo can create leads directly from the places
prospects reach you:

- **Website forms:** a contact or enquiry form on your site can create a lead automatically, with the
  details the visitor entered.
- **Incoming email:** emails to a sales address can be turned into leads, so nothing sits unnoticed in an
  inbox.
- **Manual entry:** your team can add leads from calls, events or referrals whenever they come up.

Capturing leads at the source means fewer opportunities lost and less re-keying, and every enquiry lands
in the same place ready to be worked.

## Enriching what you know

Once a lead exists, the more you know about it, the better you can qualify and prioritise it. Odoo can help
add useful context to a lead, so your team is not starting from just a name and an email. Better
information up front means smarter decisions about which leads to chase first.

## Lead mining: going out to find prospects

Sometimes you do not want to wait for leads to come to you. Lead mining lets you generate new prospects
that match a profile you care about, for example companies of a certain size, industry or location. It is
a way to proactively fill your pipeline with the kinds of prospects you want, rather than only working
inbound interest.

Used thoughtfully, this turns an empty quiet spell into an opportunity to reach out to fresh, relevant
prospects.

## Keeping it organised

However leads arrive, the value comes from handling them consistently: capturing them in one place,
qualifying them, and moving the good ones into your pipeline as opportunities. A steady, organised intake
is what keeps the whole sales engine fed.

## Practical tips

- Connect your website forms and sales email to the CRM so capture is automatic.
- Respond to new leads quickly; speed is one of the biggest factors in winning them.
- Qualify honestly, so your pipeline reflects real opportunities, not every stray enquiry.
- Use lead mining to top up the pipeline deliberately, targeting the prospects that fit you best.

## My take

Lead generation is the fuel of your sales engine. Let Odoo capture leads automatically from your website
and email, enrich them so you know who is worth pursuing, and use lead mining to go find more when you need
them. Keep the intake organised, and your pipeline never runs dry.
