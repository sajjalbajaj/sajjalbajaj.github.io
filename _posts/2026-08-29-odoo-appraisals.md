Performance reviews are easy to put off and easy to do badly: irregular, unstructured and forgotten as soon
as they finish. Odoo Appraisals gives them a proper structure. Continuing the series on Odoo HR and People,
here is appraisals.

## A consistent review process

Appraisals lets you run structured performance reviews on a regular basis, rather than as an occasional
scramble. A consistent process, with clear questions and a set rhythm, means everyone is reviewed fairly and
nothing important is skipped.

## Input from both sides

Good reviews are a conversation, not a verdict. Odoo supports gathering input from the employee as well as
the manager, so a review reflects both perspectives. That makes it fairer and more useful, and it helps
people feel heard rather than judged.

## A record over time

Because appraisals are recorded in the system, you build a history for each employee. You can look back at
past reviews, see how someone has developed, and follow up on goals set previously. Reviews stop being
one-off events and become part of an ongoing development story.

## Linked to goals and growth

Appraisals are most valuable when they lead somewhere: goals, development, recognition. Keeping them in
Odoo, alongside the rest of the employee record, means the outcomes of a review connect to the person's
ongoing information rather than disappearing into a drawer.

## Fairer and less stressful

A clear, consistent, two-way process takes much of the anxiety and arbitrariness out of reviews. Employees
know what to expect and that the process is even-handed, and managers have a structure to follow rather than
starting from a blank page.

## Practical tips

- Set a regular review cadence and stick to it.
- Gather input from both the employee and the manager.
- Use the history to follow up on goals and track development.
- Make sure reviews lead to something: goals, growth or recognition.

## My take

Appraisals turns performance reviews from an awkward, irregular chore into a structured, fair and useful
process with a record over time. Done well, reviews help people grow and feel valued, and Odoo gives you the
structure to do them consistently.
