The print, sign, scan and email dance is one of the most tediously outdated things businesses still do. It
is slow, it holds up deals, and it clutters everyone's day. Odoo Sign replaces it with electronic
signatures. Continuing the series on Odoo Productivity, here is Sign.

## Send documents for signature

Sign lets you send a document, a contract, quote, agreement or form, to someone for electronic signature.
They sign online, from any device, and it comes back signed. No printer, no scanner, no posting anything.

## Faster turnaround

The biggest win is speed. Instead of waiting days for a document to be printed, signed and returned, you can
have it signed in minutes. For anything that gates a deal or a start of work, that speed directly moves
things forward.

## Reusable templates

For documents you send often, Sign lets you set up templates, so preparing them for signature is quick and
consistent. You are not rebuilding the same contract each time; you send a ready template and fill in the
specifics.

## A clear, tracked record

Sign keeps a record of what was sent, to whom, and when it was signed. That gives you a clear audit trail
and one place to find signed documents, rather than hunting through email for the final version.

## Connected to your work

Because Sign is part of Odoo, signing connects to the rest of your processes, a signed agreement can sit with
the customer, the deal or the project it belongs to. Signature becomes a step in your workflow, not a
detour out of it.

## Practical tips

- Move your regularly signed documents onto templates for speed.
- Send for e-signature rather than print-sign-scan wherever it is accepted.
- Use the audit trail to keep signed documents organised and findable.
- Connect signing to the deal or record it relates to.

## My take

Sign removes one of those small, universal frictions that quietly slow every business down. Faster
signatures mean faster deals and less admin, and keeping it in Odoo means the signed document lands where it
belongs. A simple app that saves time on almost every agreement you make.
