A text message lands where almost nothing else does: directly on the phone, usually read within minutes.
Used well, SMS is a powerful, immediate channel. Continuing the series on Odoo Marketing, here is SMS
Marketing.

## Why SMS works

SMS has extremely high open rates and immediacy. For time-sensitive messages, an appointment reminder, an
order update, a flash offer, a text often beats email because it is seen almost straight away. That
directness is its strength, and also why it must be used with respect.

## Send campaigns from your existing data

Like email, Odoo SMS Marketing draws on the contacts and data already in your system. You target the right
people using what you already know about them, and send from the same place you manage everything else, no
separate list juggling.

## Best used sparingly and relevantly

Because SMS is so direct, it is easy to overdo. The businesses that get value from it send messages that are
timely, relevant and welcome, not constant promotions. Treat the channel with care and customers stay
subscribed and responsive.

## Great for the immediate and the timely

SMS shines for messages where timing matters: reminders, confirmations, short-window offers and important
updates. It complements email rather than replacing it, longer, richer content by email, short and urgent by
SMS.

## Measured and connected

Odoo tracks how SMS campaigns perform and, like the rest of marketing, connects to your wider data. So SMS
is not a disconnected experiment; it is part of the same measured, joined-up approach as your other
channels.

## Practical tips

- Use SMS for timely, relevant messages, not constant promotion.
- Respect consent and make it easy to opt out.
- Keep messages short, clear and with a single purpose.
- Combine with email: rich content by email, urgent nudges by SMS.

## My take

SMS Marketing is a direct, immediate channel that works best used sparingly and relevantly. Odoo lets you
run it from the same data and system as the rest of your marketing, so it is targeted and measured rather
than a standalone gamble. Respect the channel and it rewards you.
