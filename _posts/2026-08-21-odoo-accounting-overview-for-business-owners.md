Accounting is where a business finally sees the truth about itself: what it earns, what it spends, and
what it is worth. Odoo Accounting is a full, modern accounting system, and because it sits inside the same
platform as your sales, purchasing and stock, a lot of the work happens on its own. Here is the overview
for business owners, without the jargon.

## What Odoo Accounting covers

At its core, Odoo Accounting handles the money side of everything: customer invoices and the payments that
follow, vendor bills and paying suppliers, taxes, bank transactions, and the reports that tell you how the
business is doing. It is a complete double-entry accounting system, but you do not need to be an accountant
to get value from it.

## The big advantage: it is connected

Here is what makes Odoo Accounting different from a standalone package. Because it lives in the same system
as the rest of your business, entries flow in automatically:

- Confirm a **sales order**, deliver it, and the customer invoice is ready, no re-typing.
- Receive a **purchase**, and the vendor bill links to the order and receipt.
- Move **stock**, and, with automated valuation, the accounting follows.

Instead of your bookkeeper re-entering everything from other systems, the numbers arrive already connected.
That is less work, fewer errors, and a picture that is always up to date.

## Less manual entry, more automation

Odoo leans hard into removing manual work. Invoices generate from orders. Bank statements can be imported
and largely reconciled automatically. Vendor bills can be read from PDFs. The goal is that your team reviews
and confirms, rather than keys in everything by hand. That is where the real time savings live.

## It still respects proper accounting

None of this means cutting corners. Underneath the friendly surface, Odoo keeps proper double-entry books,
with a chart of accounts, journals and all the structure an accountant expects. You get ease of use on top
and accounting rigour underneath, which is exactly the right combination.

## What this series will cover

Over the next posts we will walk through the parts that matter most: customer invoicing and payments,

## Practical tips

- Get your chart of accounts and tax setup right early; they underpin everything.
- Lean on the connections to sales, purchasing and inventory rather than entering things twice.
- Automate what you can, bank imports, bill reading, so your team reviews rather than types.
- Work with your accountant on setup, then let the system do the heavy lifting day to day.

## My take

Odoo Accounting gives you real, proper accounting with far less manual effort, because it is wired into the
rest of your business. Set the foundations up well with your accountant, let the connections and automation
do the routine work, and you get finances that are accurate, current and genuinely useful for running the
company.
