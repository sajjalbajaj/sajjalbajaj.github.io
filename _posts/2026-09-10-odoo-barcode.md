In a busy warehouse, typing is the enemy: it is slow and it introduces errors. Scanning a barcode is fast and
accurate. The Odoo Barcode app brings that speed to your stock operations. Continuing this run through more
Odoo apps, here is Barcode.

## Scan instead of type

The Barcode app lets warehouse staff use a scanner to do their work: receiving goods, picking orders, packing,
transfers and inventory counts. Instead of finding a product on screen and typing quantities, they scan, which
is far quicker and much less error-prone.

## Fewer mistakes

Manual entry in a warehouse means wrong products, wrong quantities and miscounts, each of which costs time and
money to fix. Scanning confirms exactly which product and how many, so the stock movement matches reality. In
inventory, accuracy is everything, and barcodes protect it.

## Faster receiving and picking

Receiving a delivery and picking an order are the highest-volume warehouse tasks, and they are where scanning
saves the most. Staff move through the work faster, orders go out quicker, and the whole operation flows better
at higher volume.

## Easier stock counts

Counting stock by hand is slow and painful. With barcodes, counts are faster and more accurate, so you can
check stock more often and trust the numbers. Better counts mean fewer surprises and more reliable inventory
data across the business.

## Part of your live inventory

Every scan updates the same live inventory the rest of Odoo uses. So the speed and accuracy of the warehouse
feed straight into accurate stock figures for sales, purchasing and planning. The warehouse floor and the rest
of the business stay in sync.

## Practical tips

- Use scanning for receiving, picking, packing, transfers and counts.
- Make sure your products are properly barcoded to get the benefit.
- Lean on barcodes for stock counts to keep inventory accurate.
- Remember every scan feeds your live, shared stock data.

## My take

Barcode is one of those apps whose value is immediate and obvious: scan instead of type, and warehouse work
gets both faster and more accurate at once. Because every scan updates your live inventory, that accuracy
flows through the whole business. For any operation with real stock volume, it pays for itself quickly.
