Selling online is no longer optional for many businesses, but bolting a separate webshop onto a business
that runs elsewhere creates a whole new syncing problem. Odoo eCommerce avoids that by making your shop
part of the same system as your stock, sales and accounts. Continuing the series on Odoo Website and
eCommerce, here is setting up your store.

## Your shop is built on your website

Odoo eCommerce sits on top of Odoo Website, so you build your shop with the same drag-and-drop tools. Your
online store shares the look and feel of your site, and product pages are just pages you can design and
enrich.

## Products come from your catalog

This is the crucial part. The products in your shop are the same products in your Odoo inventory and sales.
You do not maintain a separate webshop catalog; you publish products from your existing catalog to the
website. Prices, descriptions and, importantly, stock all come from one place.

## Stock stays accurate automatically

Because the shop draws on your real inventory, stock levels reflect reality. When something sells online,
it is deducted from the same stock your other sales channels use, so you are far less likely to oversell.
No manual syncing between a webshop and a back office.

## Orders flow straight through

An online order in Odoo becomes a normal sales order in the same system: it can trigger a delivery, feed
into your accounts, and appear alongside orders from every other channel. One place to manage everything,
rather than logging into a separate platform and copying orders across.

## Start focused

You do not need your entire range online on day one. Start with a focused set of products, get the
buying experience right, and expand as you learn what sells and how customers behave.

## Practical tips

- Publish products from your existing catalog rather than building a separate one.
- Keep product information clear: good images, honest descriptions, correct prices.
- Trust the shared stock, so online and offline never fight over the same inventory.
- Launch focused, then expand your range as you go.

## My take

The strength of Odoo eCommerce is not that it is a shop, plenty of tools sell online. It is that the shop
is part of your business, sharing stock, orders and accounts with everything else. That connection removes
the syncing headaches that plague bolt-on webshops. Set it up on your real catalog and let it run as one
system.
