Most of what a business knows lives in people's heads and scattered documents, which is risky and
inefficient. When someone leaves or is away, the knowledge goes with them. Odoo Knowledge gives that
information a home. Continuing the series on Odoo Productivity, here is Knowledge.

## A living company wiki

Knowledge is a place to write down and organise what your business knows: processes, guides, policies,
onboarding material, how-tos. It works like a wiki, structured, editable, searchable, so your collective
knowledge is captured rather than trapped in individuals or buried in files.

## Organised and findable

Information is only useful if people can find it. Knowledge lets you organise articles into a sensible
structure and search across them, so the answer to "how do we do this" is a quick look-up rather than a
hunt or an interruption to a colleague.

## Easy to write and keep current

Because it is easy to create and edit articles, Knowledge is more likely to stay up to date, which is the
hard part of any documentation. Living documentation that reflects how you actually work beats a pristine
manual nobody maintains.

## Connected to your work

Being part of Odoo, Knowledge can link to and sit alongside the rest of your system, so guidance is close to
the work it describes. Documentation is not a separate island; it supports the actual processes running in
Odoo.

## Why it matters

Capturing knowledge protects the business: it makes onboarding faster, reduces dependence on any one person,
keeps processes consistent, and saves the constant re-explaining of the same things. It is one of those
quiet investments that pays off steadily.

## Practical tips

- Start with the knowledge that is most often asked for or most at risk.
- Organise articles clearly so answers are easy to find.
- Keep it current, living documentation beats a perfect but stale manual.
- Point people to Knowledge rather than re-explaining the same things.

## My take

Knowledge turns what your business knows from something fragile and scattered into something written,
organised and findable. That protects you against knowledge walking out the door, speeds up onboarding, and
keeps everyone working the same way. For a growing team, it is genuinely valuable.
