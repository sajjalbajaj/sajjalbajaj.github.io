HR data underpins more than people realise: payroll, time off, appraisals, even project costing all lean on it. When
the HR side is set up carelessly, the effects ripple outward. Continuing this series on common Odoo mistakes, here is
HR.

## Incomplete employee records

Everything in HR builds on the employee record, and thin, half-finished records undermine all of it. Missing contracts,
managers, departments or work schedules mean time off, payroll and reporting all go wrong downstream. Get the employee
records complete and accurate first; they are the foundation the rest stands on.

## Not using employee self-service

When HR does everything manually, filing time-off requests, updating details, chasing documents, it drowns in admin
that employees could handle themselves. Odoo's self-service lets people request leave and manage their own information.
Not using it wastes the whole team's time and makes HR a bottleneck.

## Time off set up loosely

Leave rules that do not match your real policies cause endless corrections and disputes. Different leave types,
allocations, approvals and public holidays all need to reflect how your business actually works. Setting time off up
properly once saves a running stream of manual fixes and awkward conversations.

## Treating HR as an island

HR data feeds payroll, project costs and planning, yet teams often run it in isolation. When the same employee data
serves the whole system, everything downstream gets more accurate. Treating HR as connected, rather than a separate
records department, is where its real value appears.

## Ignoring the data-privacy angle

Employee data is sensitive, and loose access to it is a real risk. Failing to think about who can see what, salaries,
personal details, documents, is a common oversight. Set access rights so HR information is seen only by those who should
see it. It protects both people and the business.

## Practical tips

- Make employee records complete: contracts, managers, schedules, departments.
- Turn on self-service so people manage their own requests and details.
- Set time off up to match your real leave policies from the start.
- Treat HR data as feeding payroll, costing and planning, not an island.
- Control access to sensitive employee information carefully.

## My take

HR mistakes are quiet but far-reaching, because so much else depends on people data. Complete records, real
self-service, properly configured time off and sensible access controls are what make the HR apps trustworthy and
useful. Get the foundation right and everything built on it, payroll included, gets easier.
