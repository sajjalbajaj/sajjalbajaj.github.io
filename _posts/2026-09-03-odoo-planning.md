Knowing what needs doing is only half of delivery; the other half is knowing who will do it and when. Odoo
Planning is where you schedule your people and resources. Continuing the series on Odoo Services and
Project, here is planning.

## Scheduling made visual

Planning gives you a visual schedule of who is working on what and when. Instead of juggling availability in
your head or a spreadsheet, you see the plan laid out, shifts, assignments and resources, and can adjust it
easily as things change.

## Assign work clearly

You can assign work to the right people for the right periods, so everyone knows what they are expected to do
and when. Clear assignments prevent the confusion and double-booking that happen when scheduling lives
informally.

## Balance the workload

One of Planning's real benefits is seeing workload across your team. You can spot who is overloaded and who
has capacity, and balance the work accordingly. That protects your people from burnout and makes sure
capacity is not wasted.

## Plan resources, not just people

Planning is not only about staff. It can cover the resources work depends on, so you schedule around what is
actually available. That keeps plans realistic rather than optimistic.

## Connected to delivery

Because Planning sits in Odoo alongside Project and Timesheets, scheduling connects to the actual work and
the time it takes. Plan the work, do the work, track the time, all in one system, so your planning is
informed by reality and improves over time.

## Practical tips

- Keep the schedule visual and current so everyone can rely on it.
- Assign work clearly, with the right person for the right period.
- Watch workload across the team and rebalance before people burn out.
- Plan around real availability of both people and resources.

## My take

Planning turns good intentions into an actual, visible schedule. It answers the who and when that delivery
depends on, helps you balance workload fairly, and connects to the rest of your project and time data. For
any team juggling multiple pieces of work, that clarity is worth a great deal.
