CRM and Sales are where revenue is won or lost, so mistakes here cost real money, usually quietly, in deals that go cold
rather than errors you can see. Most of them are habits rather than settings. Continuing this series on common Odoo
mistakes, here is CRM and Sales.

## An overcomplicated pipeline

Teams love to build elaborate pipelines with a dozen stages, and then nobody keeps them updated because it is too much
work. A pipeline that does not reflect reality is worse than a simple one. Keep your stages few and meaningful, so the
pipeline stays current and actually tells you something.

## Neglecting follow-up activities

The most common sales mistake in any system is the follow-up that never happens. Odoo makes it easy to schedule the
next step as an activity, but only if people use it. A pipeline full of opportunities with no scheduled next action is a
pipeline slowly going cold. Every open opportunity should have a next step with a date.

## Not logging what happened

When calls and meetings are not logged, the history of a relationship lives only in someone's head, and vanishes when
they are busy or leave. Logging outcomes takes seconds and means anyone can pick up the thread. A CRM is only as good as
what people put into it.

## Dirty contact data

Duplicate customers, misspelt companies and out-of-date details make the CRM frustrating and the reporting unreliable.
Sales teams then stop trusting it. Keeping contact data clean, with tools like Data Cleaning, is what keeps the CRM
worth using rather than something people work around.

## Ignoring the reporting

Odoo can tell you your win rate, your pipeline value, where deals stall, but only if you look. Teams that never use the
reporting are flying blind and missing the patterns that would help them sell better. The data is there; the mistake is
not using it.

## Practical tips

- Keep your pipeline stages few and meaningful so they stay current.
- Give every open opportunity a scheduled next activity.
- Log calls and meetings so the relationship history survives.
- Keep contact data clean so the team trusts the CRM.
- Actually use the reporting to see where deals win and stall.

## My take

CRM mistakes are rarely technical; they are about discipline. The system makes it easy to keep a clean pipeline, log
activity and schedule follow-ups, but it cannot do those things for you. Teams that build the habit of updating as they
go get a CRM that genuinely helps them sell. Teams that do not get an expensive contact list.
