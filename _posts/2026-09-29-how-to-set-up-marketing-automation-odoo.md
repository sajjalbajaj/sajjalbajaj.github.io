Marketing automation lets you send the right message to the right person at the right time without doing it by hand each
time. Odoo Marketing Automation builds these journeys on your own contact data. Setting it up thoughtfully is what makes
it effective rather than annoying. Continuing this series of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is marketing automation.

## Start with your target audience

Every automation begins with who it is for. Set up your target audience using the segmentation Odoo can do on your
contact data: new leads, customers who bought a certain product, people in a region. Getting the audience right is the
foundation, because sending even a perfect message to the wrong people helps no one.

## Design the automated journey

An automation is a sequence of steps triggered by what people do or by time passing: send a welcome email, wait a few
days, send a follow-up if they did not respond. Design the journey to reflect a sensible, human sequence of
communication. Map out what you want to happen before you build it, step by step.

## Use triggers and conditions

The power of automation is in reacting to behaviour. Set up triggers and conditions so the journey adapts: if someone
opens an email, do one thing; if they do not, do another. This is what makes automation feel relevant rather than
robotic, because people receive messages that respond to what they actually did.

## Keep it relevant and human

The biggest risk with automation is becoming impersonal or overbearing. Set sensible timing and frequency, and write
messages that sound like a person, not a machine. Relevance and restraint are what keep automation welcome. Bombarding
people or sending obviously irrelevant messages damages your reputation and your deliverability.

## Monitor and refine

Automation is not set-and-forget. Once running, watch how your journeys perform, open rates, clicks, conversions, and
refine them. Business and audiences change, and journeys drift out of date if ignored. Treating automation as something
you tend and improve is what keeps it working over time.

## Practical tips

- Define a clear target audience using segmentation first.
- Design the journey as a sensible, human sequence of steps.
- Use triggers and conditions so it responds to behaviour.
- Keep timing, frequency and tone relevant and human.
- Monitor performance and refine journeys regularly.

## My take

Marketing automation is powerful precisely because it acts on your real contact data and people's behaviour, sending
relevant messages without constant manual effort. The key is setting it up thoughtfully: the right audience, a
human-feeling journey, behaviour-based triggers and ongoing refinement. Done with care it is a genuine multiplier; done
carelessly it just annoys people, so the setup mindset matters as much as the tool.
