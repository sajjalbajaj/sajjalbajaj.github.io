Equipment that breaks down unexpectedly disrupts everything, and neglected upkeep leads straight to breakdowns. Odoo
Maintenance helps you look after your machines and assets with both planned and reactive maintenance. Setting it up well
protects your operations. To close out this run of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is Maintenance.

## Set up your equipment

Start by adding the equipment and assets you want to maintain: machines, tools, vehicles, whatever matters to your
operation. Each becomes a record you can track maintenance against. Setting up your equipment accurately is the
foundation, because all the maintenance you plan and record attaches to these individual assets.

## Plan preventive maintenance

The real value of Maintenance is preventive care: servicing equipment on a schedule before it fails. Set up preventive
maintenance so upkeep happens at the right intervals, by time or by usage. Planned maintenance is far cheaper than
emergency repairs and unexpected downtime, so this is where the app earns its keep.

## Handle breakdowns with maintenance requests

When something does go wrong, set up maintenance requests so breakdowns are reported, tracked and resolved rather than
handled in a scramble. A clear process for corrective maintenance means problems get to the right people and are recorded,
so you also build up a history of what fails and why.

## Give your maintenance team a clear view

Set up how your maintenance team sees and works their tasks, both scheduled upkeep and incoming requests. A clear view of
what needs doing keeps maintenance organised and nothing forgotten. When the team can see their workload at a glance,
planned work actually gets done rather than being pushed aside by whatever broke today.

## Use the data to improve

Over time, maintenance records show you which equipment is reliable and which keeps causing trouble. Set up how you review
this so you can act on it: service problem machines more, or plan to replace them. Using the maintenance history to make
decisions is what turns upkeep from pure cost into smarter asset management.

## Practical tips

- Add the equipment and assets you want to maintain.
- Plan preventive maintenance by time or usage to prevent failures.
- Use maintenance requests to handle breakdowns in an orderly way.
- Give the maintenance team a clear view of scheduled and reactive work.
- Use maintenance history to decide what to service more or replace.

## My take

Maintenance shifts equipment care from reactive firefighting to planned upkeep, which is almost always cheaper and less
disruptive. Well-recorded equipment, scheduled preventive maintenance, an orderly way to handle breakdowns and use of the
resulting data are what make it work. For any business that depends on machines or assets, that shift protects both your
operations and your budget.
