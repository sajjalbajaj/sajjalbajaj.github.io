Most business chat happens away from the work it is about, in a separate messaging app, disconnected from
the records it concerns. Odoo Discuss keeps communication where the work is. This starts a short series on
Odoo Productivity, the final part of the Odoo guide series.

## Channels and direct messages

Discuss gives you channels for teams and topics, and direct messages between people, much like the chat
tools you already know. So everyday communication has a home inside the same system you run the business
on, rather than a separate app.

## The real difference: context

Here is what sets Discuss apart. Because it is part of Odoo, conversations can be tied to the actual
records they are about, a sales order, a project task, a customer. The discussion lives with the thing it
concerns, so the context is never lost and anyone looking at the record sees the relevant conversation.

## The chatter on every record

Across Odoo, records have a "chatter", a running log of messages, notes and changes. That means the history
of a deal, task or order, who said what and what happened, travels with it. No more digging through a
separate chat app to find that one decision.

## Notifications that matter

Because Odoo knows what you are involved in, it can notify you about the things that concern you, an
assigned task, a message, a follow-up, so you stay on top of your work without drowning in noise.

## Less tool-switching

Keeping communication inside Odoo means less jumping between apps and less context lost in the gap. The
conversation, the work and the record are together, which is quietly more efficient than stitching separate
tools together.

## Practical tips

- Use channels for teams and topics to keep discussion organised.
- Lean on the chatter so decisions and history live with the record.
- Let Odoo notify you about what you are involved in.
- Keep work-related conversation in context rather than a separate app.

## My take

Discuss is not trying to replace every chat tool, but by keeping communication tied to the actual work and
records, it removes the disconnect that separate messaging apps create. When the conversation lives with the
work, context is preserved and less falls through the cracks.
