For Indian businesses, a new Odoo version raises two specific questions: when can we get it here, and does it still
handle everything Indian compliance demands? Here is a clear, India-focused preview of Odoo 20.

A note on accuracy: as of early September 2026, Odoo 20 has not launched. The points below are based on Odoo's public
roadmap and its established India localization, with the final details confirmed at launch.

## When is Odoo 20 available in India?

The good news is simple: at the same time as everywhere else. Odoo releases new versions globally in one go, so there is
no separate, delayed India launch. Odoo 20 is unveiled at Odoo Experience 2026 in late September 2026, with the stable,
production-ready version following a few weeks later, into October or November. Indian businesses are on exactly the same
timeline as the rest of the world.

## Indian compliance carries forward

The most important reassurance for Indian users is that the India localization continues in Odoo 20. Everything Indian
businesses depend on is part of Odoo's India support and carries through to the new version: GST with automatic CGST,
SGST, IGST and cess based on fiscal positions; e-invoicing integrated with the government IRP for IRN generation; e-way
bill generation; GSTR reporting; GSTR-2B matching for input tax credit; and TDS and TCS handling within the accounting
workflow.

## Indian payroll too

Odoo's India support goes beyond tax. Indian payroll, with provident fund, ESI and professional tax, is part of the
localization, so businesses can run compliant Indian salary structures. As with the tax features, this is maintained
version to version, so moving to Odoo 20 does not mean losing the Indian payroll support you rely on.

## What Indian businesses gain from Odoo 20

Beyond compliance, Indian businesses get everything the new version brings: the move toward smarter, more automated
workflows, a redesigned mobile experience, and improvements across accounting, CRM, inventory and more. For fast-growing
Indian SMEs in particular, a system that is both locally compliant and getting genuinely smarter is a strong combination.

## A practical tip on localization setup

One important, often-missed point for Indian implementations: the India localization should be installed on your
production database before you start entering transactions. Applying it cleanly to a database that already has financial
entries is difficult and usually needs professional data work. So if you are starting fresh on Odoo, get the India
localization in place first.

## My take

For Indian businesses, Odoo 20 is reassuring on the thing that matters most, compliance, while bringing the same
forward-looking improvements everyone else gets. The GST, e-invoicing, e-way bill, TDS, TCS and Indian payroll support
carry forward, and you are on the same global timeline for the new version. The advice is the same as anywhere: plan the
upgrade for after the first stable patch, and get it set up correctly, especially the localization.
