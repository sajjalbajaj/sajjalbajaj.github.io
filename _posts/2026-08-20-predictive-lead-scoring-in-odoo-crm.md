Not all leads are equal, but sales teams often treat them as if they are, working whatever is newest or
loudest. Predictive lead scoring in Odoo CRM fixes that by ranking leads on how likely they are to convert,
so effort goes where it pays off. Here is how it works, in plain English.

## What lead scoring does

Predictive lead scoring gives each lead a score based on how similar it is to the deals you have actually
won in the past. Odoo looks at your own historical data, which leads turned into customers and which did
not, and learns the patterns. New leads are then scored on the same basis.

The result is a ranked list: the leads most likely to convert rise to the top. Your team can start their day
with the best opportunities instead of guessing.

## Based on your data, not gut feel

The important part is that this is grounded in your real history, not a generic formula or someone's hunch.
The patterns come from your own wins and losses, so the scoring reflects how your business actually converts
leads. As you close more deals, the picture only gets richer.

## Why it matters

Sales time is your scarcest resource. Spend it evenly across every lead and you dilute your effort on
prospects that were never going to buy. Focus it on the highest-scoring leads and you win more from the same
hours. Lead scoring is simply a way to point your team's energy where it has the best chance of paying off.

It also helps prioritise fairly and consistently, rather than the loudest or newest lead always getting
attention regardless of quality.

## Using it well

Scoring is a guide, not a rule. A few sensible habits:

- Treat the score as a prioritisation aid, not an absolute verdict; judgement still matters.
- Do not completely ignore lower-scored leads; some still convert, and quick wins hide there.
- Keep your CRM data clean and complete, because the scoring is only as good as the data behind it.
- Revisit how well the scoring matches reality as your business and market change.

## The bigger picture

Lead scoring is one of the ways AI is quietly becoming useful inside everyday business tools. It does not
replace your salespeople; it points them at the right doors to knock on first. That is exactly the kind of
practical, in-the-flow help that makes AI worth having.

## My take

Predictive lead scoring turns your own sales history into a simple, powerful advantage: a ranked list of who
to call first. Use it to focus your team's limited time on the leads most likely to convert, keep your data
clean so the scores stay honest, and let judgement ride alongside the numbers.
