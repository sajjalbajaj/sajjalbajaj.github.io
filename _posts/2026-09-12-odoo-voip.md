For sales and support teams, the phone is still central, and yet the call and the system where you record it are
usually two separate worlds. Odoo VoIP joins them. Continuing this run through more Odoo apps, here is VoIP.

## Calling from inside Odoo

VoIP lets you place calls directly from Odoo. Click the number on a contact, a lead or a ticket, and the call is
placed, so there is no copying a number into a separate phone. The record you are looking at and the call you are
making are the same action.

## Calls tied to your records

Because the call happens from within Odoo, it is linked to the right contact or opportunity. You can log what was said,
schedule the follow-up, and see the call in the record's history. Nobody has to remember to write it up separately, so
the history of a relationship actually stays complete.

## Better for sales and support

For a sales team working a pipeline, calling from the opportunity and logging the outcome in one motion is a real time
saver. For support, having calls attached to the customer and their tickets means the next person has context. The
value is in the phone being part of the workflow rather than beside it.

## A queue you can work through

VoIP gives you a call queue, so you can line up the people you need to reach and work through them efficiently. For
anyone doing a round of calls, that structure beats hunting for each number one at a time.

## Part of the connected system

The recurring theme holds here too: the phone becomes part of the same system as your contacts, sales and support,
rather than a separate device with its own disconnected notes. Communication and records live together.

## Practical tips

- Call directly from the contact, lead or ticket so the call is always linked.
- Log outcomes and schedule follow-ups in the same motion as the call.
- Use the call queue when you have a batch of people to reach.
- Treat the phone as part of your CRM workflow, not a separate tool.

## My take

VoIP is most valuable for teams that live on the phone. By making and logging calls from inside Odoo, it keeps the
record of every conversation attached to the right person automatically, which is exactly the part that usually gets
lost. Less admin, better history, and the phone finally part of the same system as everything else.
