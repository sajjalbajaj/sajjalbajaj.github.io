A website used to mean a developer, a content management system and a lot of back and forth. Odoo turns
it into something you can build yourself, visually, inside the same system that runs your business. This
starts a short series on Odoo Website and eCommerce.

## The drag-and-drop builder

The heart of Odoo Website is a visual, drag-and-drop builder. You choose from ready-made building blocks,
headers, text, images, features, calls to action, and drop them onto the page, then edit the content in
place. What you see as you build is what visitors get.

That means you do not need to write code or wait on a developer for everyday changes. Updating a page,
adding a section or launching a new landing page is something your own team can do in minutes.

## Themes and a consistent look

Odoo lets you set a theme and style, colours, fonts and spacing, once, so every page stays on-brand
without fiddling. A consistent, professional look across the site builds trust, and it comes without
designing each page from scratch.

## Mobile-friendly by default

Visitors arrive on phones as much as desktops, and a site that breaks on mobile loses them. Odoo's
building blocks are responsive, so your pages adapt to the screen automatically, and you can preview how a
page looks on different devices as you build.

## The real advantage: it is part of your ERP

Here is what sets Odoo Website apart from a standalone site builder. It lives in the same system as your
sales, inventory, CRM and more. A contact form can create a lead in your CRM. A product on your site draws
on your real stock. An event or appointment ties into the rest of Odoo. The website is not a separate
island; it is a front door to your connected business.

## Start simple, grow later

You do not need a huge site on day one. Start with the essential pages, home, about, services or products,
and contact, get them clean and clear, then add more as you go. The builder makes expanding easy, so the
site can grow with you.

## Practical tips

- Plan your key pages and their purpose before you start building.
- Set your theme and brand style once, then keep pages consistent.
- Always check how pages look on mobile.
- Use forms that feed into your CRM, so enquiries are captured, not lost.

## My take

Odoo Website puts a capable, professional site within reach without a developer for every change, and
because it is part of your ERP, it connects straight into how your business actually runs. Start with the
essentials, keep it on-brand, and let it grow.
