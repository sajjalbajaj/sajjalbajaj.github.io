If your business sends people out to do work on site, installs, repairs, maintenance, Field Service coordinates the whole
thing from scheduling to invoicing. Setting it up well is what connects the office and the technician in the field.
Continuing this series of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is Field Service.

## Set up your services and tasks

Start with the kinds of on-site work you do, set up as services or task types. Each job that goes to the field becomes a
task with the details a technician needs. Setting these up clearly means the person on site knows what they are there to
do, which is the foundation of a smooth field operation.

## Schedule and dispatch work

The heart of Field Service is getting the right person to the right place at the right time. Set up scheduling so jobs
are assigned to technicians with the availability and skills to do them. Good scheduling avoids both idle time and
overload, and makes sure customers are served when promised. This coordination is where much of the value lives.

## Equip technicians for the field

Technicians need their job information on the move: what to do, where, for whom, and the ability to record what they did.
Set this up so they can work from a phone or tablet on site, updating the job as they go. A technician who can see and
update the job in the field is what keeps the office and the site in step.

## Capture work done and materials used

On site, technicians record the time they spent and any materials or parts they used. Setting this up matters because it
feeds both the record of the job and the invoice. Capturing this at the point of work, rather than reconstructing it
later, is what makes billing accurate and complete.

## Connect to invoicing

Field Service connects the work done to invoicing, so the time and materials recorded on site turn into a bill. Setting
up this link means you charge accurately for what was actually done, and no billable work slips through. The flow from
completed job to invoice is what turns field work into properly captured revenue.

## Practical tips

- Set up your on-site services and task types clearly.
- Schedule and dispatch work to the right technicians.
- Equip technicians to see and update jobs from the field.
- Capture time and materials at the point of work.
- Connect completed jobs to invoicing for accurate billing.

## My take

Field Service shines by connecting the office and the field into one flow: schedule, dispatch, do the work, record it,
invoice it. Clear services, good scheduling, mobile-equipped technicians and a link to billing are what make it work. Set
up well, it removes the gaps where field businesses usually lose time and money, keeping everyone in step from the first
call to the final invoice.
