If your business runs vehicles, cars, vans, delivery trucks, they are a significant cost and
responsibility that is easy to lose track of. Odoo Fleet brings vehicle management into one organised
place. Continuing the series on Odoo HR and People, here is fleet.

## One register of your vehicles

Fleet gives you a single record of every vehicle: its details, who it is assigned to, and its status. Instead
of vehicle information living in someone's head or a spreadsheet, you have an organised register you can
actually rely on.

## Assignments to drivers

You can track which vehicle is assigned to which employee, so responsibility is clear. When vehicles move
between people, the record reflects it, and you always know who has what.

## Contracts and costs

Vehicles come with contracts, leases, insurance, and running costs, fuel, servicing, and these add up.
Odoo Fleet lets you track these, so you can see what your fleet actually costs and manage renewals and
commitments rather than being caught out by them.

## Maintenance and services

Keeping vehicles serviced protects both safety and value. Fleet helps you keep on top of maintenance and
services, so vehicles are looked after on schedule rather than reactively when something goes wrong.

## Part of the bigger picture

Because Fleet sits in Odoo, vehicle costs and assignments connect to the rest of the business, including
your people and, where relevant, your accounts. It is one more area brought into the same connected system
rather than managed on the side.

## Practical tips

- Keep an accurate register of all vehicles and their assignments.
- Track contracts and renewal dates so nothing lapses unexpectedly.
- Record running costs to understand what your fleet really costs.
- Stay ahead of maintenance and services rather than reacting to breakdowns.

## My take

Fleet is a focused app for a real need: if you run vehicles, they are too costly to manage on scraps of
paper. Odoo Fleet gives you one place for vehicles, assignments, contracts, costs and maintenance, so your
fleet is controlled rather than a source of surprises.
