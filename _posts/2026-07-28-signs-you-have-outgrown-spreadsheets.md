Spreadsheets are brilliant, until they are not. Almost every business starts on them, and many
stay too long. Here are the signs that your spreadsheets have quietly become a liability, and
what to do about it.

## 1. Nobody trusts the numbers

Multiple versions float around, people edit them by hand, and you end up with files named
"final_v3_really.xlsx". When teams argue about whose figures are correct, you have outgrown
spreadsheets. There is no single source of truth.

## 2. Too much manual copy and paste

Re-keying the same data between sheets and systems wastes hours and quietly introduces errors.
If your team spends more time maintaining spreadsheets than actually using the information in
them, that is a clear signal.

## 3. No real-time picture

By the time a report is pulled together, it is already out of date. You are steering the
business by last month's numbers instead of what is happening right now.

## 4. It breaks when one person is away

A critical spreadsheet that only one person truly understands is a business risk. If work grinds
to a halt when they are on leave, the process is too fragile to rely on.

## 5. You cannot scale

Add more products, orders, locations or people, and the sheets get slower, heavier and more
error-prone. Growth is exactly when spreadsheets start to crack.

## What to do about it

The fix is to move the core of your business onto one connected system, an ERP. That gives you
real-time data, automation, one source of truth, and no more version chaos. You do not have to
boil the ocean either. Start with the single process that hurts the most, prove the value, then
expand from there.

## My take

Spreadsheets are a great starting point and a poor operating system for a growing business. If
two or more of these signs feel familiar, it is time to look at ERP. Start small, keep it
practical, and let the system take over the repetitive work.
