Managing leave by email and spreadsheet is a small, constant drain: requests get lost, balances are
unclear, and clashes are spotted too late. Odoo Time Off makes it simple and self-service. Continuing the
series on Odoo HR and People, here is time off.

## Self-service requests

Employees can request time off themselves, choosing the type and dates, and see the status of their
request. No more emailing a manager and hoping it gets noted somewhere. The request goes into the system,
where it can be handled properly.

## Approvals that are clear

Managers get leave requests to approve or decline in one place, with the context they need. That makes
approvals quick and consistent, and it means every decision is recorded rather than living in an email
thread someone later cannot find.

## Balances everyone can see

One of the biggest frustrations with leave is not knowing how much is left. Odoo tracks balances
automatically, so both employees and managers can see allowances and what has been taken. That removes a
whole category of back-and-forth and confusion.

## Visibility of who is off

Because leave is in the system, managers and teams can see who is away and when. That helps with planning,
avoiding too many people off at once, and it feeds into the wider picture of capacity across the business.

## Connected to the rest of HR

Time off ties into attendances, payroll and planning, because it all sits in the same system. Leave taken
is reflected where it needs to be, rather than being re-entered app by app. That is the quiet benefit of
HR living inside one ERP.

## Practical tips

- Set up your leave types and allowances to match your policies.
- Let employees request leave themselves, so nothing gets lost.
- Keep approvals prompt so people can plan with certainty.
- Use the visibility of who is off to plan around capacity.

## My take

Time Off replaces a fiddly, error-prone manual process with a clear, self-service one: employees request,
managers approve, balances update automatically, and everyone can see who is away. It is a small app that
removes a surprising amount of daily friction.
