Hiring is one of the most important things a business does, and one of the easiest to let become chaotic:
CVs in inboxes, notes on paper, candidates falling through the cracks. Odoo Recruitment brings order to it.
Continuing the series on Odoo HR and People, here is recruitment.

## Posting jobs

Recruitment starts with a clear job opening. In Odoo you create job positions and can publish them to your
website's careers page, so applications come in through a proper channel rather than scattered emails. The
opening is defined once, with the details candidates and your team need.

## A pipeline for candidates

Just like a sales pipeline, Odoo gives recruitment a visual pipeline. Each candidate is a card, and the
stages, for example New, Qualified, Interview and Offer, show exactly where everyone stands. You drag
candidates through the stages as they progress, so nobody is forgotten and the whole hiring process is
visible at a glance.

## Everything about a candidate in one place

Each candidate record holds their CV, contact details and the history of your interactions. Anyone involved
in hiring can see the full picture, so interviews are informed and decisions are made on complete
information rather than half-remembered impressions.

## From candidate to employee

When you make a hire, the transition is smooth: the candidate's information can flow into an employee
record, so you are not re-typing everything you already collected. Recruitment and the Employees app work
together, closing the loop from applicant to team member.

## A better candidate experience

An organised process is not just good for you, it is good for candidates. Timely responses and a smooth
flow reflect well on your business and help you win the people you want, which matters in a competitive
market.

## Practical tips

- Write clear job positions and publish them where candidates will find them.
- Set recruitment stages that match how you actually hire.
- Keep every candidate in the pipeline so none slip through.
- Move a new hire's details into their employee record rather than re-entering them.

## My take

Recruitment done in spreadsheets and inboxes loses good candidates and wastes time. Odoo turns hiring into
an organised pipeline where every applicant is visible and every step is tracked, then hands the new hire
cleanly to the Employees app. That structure helps you hire better and faster.
