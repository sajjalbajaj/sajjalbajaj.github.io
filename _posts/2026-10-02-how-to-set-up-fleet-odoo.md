If your business runs vehicles, cars, vans, trucks, keeping track of them, their costs and their upkeep is a job in
itself. Odoo Fleet brings it all into one place. Setting it up well is what turns a scattered set of vehicle records into
clear oversight. To close out this run of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is Fleet.

## Set up your vehicles

Start by adding your vehicles, each with its details: make, model, registration, and who it is assigned to. This creates
the central record for every vehicle. Getting these set up accurately is the foundation, because all the costs,
contracts and maintenance you track hang off the individual vehicle records.

## Track costs

A big reason to use Fleet is understanding what your vehicles actually cost. Set up cost tracking for fuel, repairs,
insurance and the rest, so you can see the true running cost of each vehicle and the fleet as a whole. This visibility is
what lets you make good decisions about keeping, replacing or changing vehicles.

## Manage contracts

Vehicles come with contracts: leasing, insurance, service agreements, each with renewal dates. Set these up in Fleet so
you have them recorded and are warned before they lapse. Keeping contracts tracked means no nasty surprises from an
expired policy or an auto-renewed lease you meant to change.

## Schedule maintenance and services

Vehicles need servicing, and missed maintenance is both a safety and a cost risk. Set up Fleet to record and schedule
services so upkeep is planned rather than forgotten. Keeping vehicles properly maintained protects their value, keeps
them safe, and avoids the bigger costs that come from neglect.

## Assign vehicles to drivers

Set up which vehicle is assigned to which driver, so you always know who has what. This matters for accountability, for
allocating costs, and for practical day-to-day management. A clear record of assignments keeps the fleet organised and
makes it obvious who is responsible for each vehicle.

## Practical tips

- Add each vehicle with complete, accurate details.
- Track all costs to see the true running cost per vehicle.
- Record contracts with their renewal dates so none lapse unnoticed.
- Schedule maintenance so upkeep is planned, not forgotten.
- Assign vehicles to drivers for clear accountability.

## My take

Fleet takes the scattered, easily neglected job of managing vehicles and makes it organised and visible. Accurate vehicle
records, real cost tracking, managed contracts and scheduled maintenance are what turn a fleet from a source of surprise
bills into something you actively control. For any business with more than a couple of vehicles, that oversight quickly
pays for the small effort of setting it up.
