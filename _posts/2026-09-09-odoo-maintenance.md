Equipment that breaks down at the wrong moment can stop production, blow budgets and frustrate everyone.
Looking after it deliberately, rather than reacting when it fails, saves real money. Continuing this run
through more Odoo apps, here is Maintenance.

## Two kinds of maintenance

Maintenance comes in two flavours, and Odoo handles both. Corrective maintenance is fixing something after it
breaks. Preventive maintenance is servicing things on a schedule so they are less likely to break in the first
place. The shift from the first to the second is where the savings live.

## A register of your equipment

Maintenance starts with knowing what you have. Odoo lets you keep a record of your equipment and machines, so
there is one place that knows what exists, where it is, and its maintenance history. That history is gold when
deciding what to service, repair or replace.

## Preventive schedules

The real value is planning ahead. You can schedule preventive maintenance, so servicing happens on time rather
than being forgotten until something fails. Regular, planned upkeep keeps equipment reliable and extends its
life, which is far cheaper than emergency repairs and downtime.

## Maintenance requests

When something does go wrong, a maintenance request captures it: what is broken, on which equipment, and its
status through to resolution. Instead of a shouted "the machine is down" that gets half-remembered, you have a
tracked request that gets actioned and recorded.

## Connected to operations

Because Maintenance sits in Odoo alongside manufacturing and inventory, equipment upkeep connects to the
operations that depend on it. Well-maintained machines mean smoother production, and the maintenance history
informs decisions across the business.

## Practical tips

- Keep a clear register of equipment and its history.
- Shift effort from fixing breakdowns to preventing them with schedules.
- Capture issues as maintenance requests so nothing is forgotten.
- Use maintenance history to guide repair-or-replace decisions.

## My take

Maintenance is about trading expensive, disruptive breakdowns for cheaper, planned upkeep. Odoo gives you the
equipment register, preventive schedules and request tracking to make that shift, connected to the operations
that rely on the machines. For anyone running equipment, staying ahead of failure is money in the bank.
