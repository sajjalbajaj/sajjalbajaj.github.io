You can attract visitors and win them over on the product page, but if checkout is confusing or payment is
awkward, the sale still slips away. Checkout is where intent becomes revenue. Continuing the series on Odoo
Website and eCommerce, here is payments and checkout.

## A smooth checkout matters

Every extra step or moment of confusion at checkout costs you sales. Odoo provides a clear checkout flow
that guides the customer from cart to confirmation, capturing the details you need without making them work
for it. The simpler and more reassuring the flow, the more carts turn into orders.

## Payment providers

To take money online, you connect a payment provider. Odoo supports connecting popular payment providers,
so customers can pay by card and other common methods, securely, without leaving your site. You choose the
providers that suit your market and customers.

The key is offering the payment methods your customers actually expect, so nobody abandons a purchase
because their preferred option is missing.

## It flows into your accounts

Here is the ERP advantage again. A paid online order does not sit in a separate system waiting to be
reconciled by hand. It becomes a sales order in Odoo, and the payment ties into your accounting, so revenue
is recorded and reconciliation is far simpler. The shop and the books are part of one system.

## Building trust at the moment of payment

Customers are most cautious when handing over money. A professional, secure checkout, clear pricing with no
nasty surprises, and recognisable payment options all build the trust needed to complete the purchase.
Getting this stage right protects the revenue you worked to earn.

## Practical tips

- Keep the checkout flow short and clear, removing needless steps.
- Offer the payment methods your customers actually expect.
- Show honest, complete pricing before the final step, no surprises.
- Let the order and payment flow into Odoo accounting rather than reconciling by hand.

## My take

Checkout and payment are where all your other effort pays off or falls down. Odoo gives you a smooth
checkout, connections to the payment providers customers expect, and a direct link into your accounts.
Make the flow simple and trustworthy, and you convert more of the interest you worked hard to create.
