Managing several social media accounts by logging into each one is a scattered, time-consuming way to work. Odoo Social
Marketing brings them together, so you post and monitor from one place, connected to the rest of your business. Setting it
up well saves real time. Continuing this series of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is Social Marketing.

## Connect your social accounts

Start by connecting the social media accounts your business uses to Odoo. This is the foundation: once connected, you can
manage them from one place rather than jumping between apps and logins. Set up the accounts you actively use, so your
social activity is centralised where the rest of your work already lives.

## Plan and schedule your posts

Rather than posting ad hoc, use Social Marketing to plan and schedule content ahead of time. Setting up a schedule means
your social presence stays consistent even when you are busy, because posts go out automatically when you planned them.
Consistency is much of what makes social media work, and scheduling is how you achieve it without daily effort.

## Post to the right channels

Different messages suit different platforms, so set up your posts to go to the channels that fit. You can tailor content
per platform rather than blasting the identical post everywhere. Thinking about which channel each message belongs on is
what makes your social presence feel considered rather than automated for its own sake.

## Monitor engagement

Social Marketing lets you see how your posts perform and monitor engagement from within Odoo. Set up how you review this,
so you can see what resonates with your audience. Watching engagement turns social from a shot in the dark into something
you can learn from and improve, just like your other marketing channels.

## Connect it to your wider marketing

Because it is part of Odoo, Social Marketing sits alongside your other channels and your customer data. Set it up as part
of your overall marketing rather than an island, so social supports your broader efforts. Seeing all your channels in one
system is what lets you run marketing as a whole rather than as disconnected pieces.

## Practical tips

- Connect the social accounts your business actively uses.
- Plan and schedule posts ahead for a consistent presence.
- Tailor posts to the channels that suit each message.
- Monitor engagement to learn what resonates.
- Treat social as part of your wider, connected marketing.

## My take

Social Marketing removes the scatter of managing platforms one login at a time, bringing posting, scheduling and
monitoring into one connected place. Connected accounts, a planned schedule, channel-appropriate posts and attention to
engagement are what make it work. Sitting inside Odoo alongside your other marketing, it helps you run social as part of a
coherent whole rather than a separate, time-eating chore.
