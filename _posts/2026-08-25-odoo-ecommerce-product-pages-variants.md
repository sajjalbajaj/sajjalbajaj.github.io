In an online shop, the product page is where the sale is won or lost. It has to inform, reassure and make
buying easy. Continuing the series on Odoo Website and eCommerce, here is how Odoo handles product pages,
variants and your catalog.

## Product pages that sell

A good product page needs clear images, an honest and helpful description, the price, and an obvious way to
buy. In Odoo you design product pages with the same building blocks as the rest of your site, so you can
add the detail that helps customers decide, images, features, specifications, without needing a developer.

Because the shop is part of your ERP, the price and availability shown come straight from your real data,
so customers see accurate information.

## Variants: one product, many options

Most products come in options, size, colour, material, and cramming these into separate listings makes a
messy shop. Odoo handles this with variants: one product with selectable options on the page. The customer
picks size and colour, and Odoo knows exactly which variant they are ordering, tracking its own stock and,
where needed, its own price.

This keeps your catalog clean while giving customers real choice, and it keeps stock accurate per variant.

## Organising the catalog

Customers need to find things. Odoo lets you organise products into categories and make them searchable, so
visitors can browse or search their way to what they want. A well-structured catalog reduces the friction
between arriving and buying.

## Enriching pages to build trust

Beyond the basics, you can add the content that overcomes hesitation: detailed descriptions, comparisons,
frequently asked questions and cross-sells. The more confidently a customer understands what they are
buying, the more likely they are to buy.

## Practical tips

- Use clear, quality images and honest, helpful descriptions.
- Model real options as variants rather than separate products.
- Organise products into sensible categories and keep search working.
- Add the extra detail that answers questions and builds buying confidence.

## My take

Your product pages are your online sales team, so it is worth making them clear, informative and easy to
buy from. Odoo lets you design them yourself, handle variants cleanly, and keep the catalog organised, all
on top of accurate, ERP-connected data. Get these right and more visitors turn into customers.
