Setting up a shop with Odoo eCommerce means your online store is connected to your stock, sales and accounting from day
one. Getting the setup right is what makes it sell smoothly rather than frustrate buyers. Continuing this series of
practical Odoo how-to guides, here is setting up an online store.

## Get your products ready for the web

Your online store draws on your product data, so start there. Each product you want to sell online needs a good
description, clear images and a correct price, and it should be published to the website. Because the same product feeds
your shop and your stock, rich, accurate product data is the foundation of a store that sells.

## Design the shop pages

Odoo's website builder lets you lay out your shop, category pages and product pages without needing to code. Aim for
clean, easy navigation: shoppers should find what they want quickly and understand what they are buying. A tidy,
trustworthy-looking shop converts far better than a cluttered one, so spend a little care on the layout.

## Set up payment

To take money online, you connect a payment provider so customers can pay at checkout. Set this up and test it
thoroughly, because a broken or confusing payment step is where sales die. Offering the payment methods your customers
actually expect, and making checkout smooth, is what turns browsers into buyers.

## Configure delivery

Shoppers need to know how and when they will get their order and what it costs. Set up your delivery methods and
shipping charges so the options are clear at checkout. Surprises about delivery cost or time are a leading cause of
abandoned carts, so make this honest and upfront.

## Keep stock accurate

Because the store is connected to your inventory, accurate stock is what lets it show the truth: what is available and
what is not. This link is a major reason to run eCommerce on Odoo rather than a separate platform. Keep stock accurate
and the shop never sells what you cannot deliver.

## Practical tips

- Prepare products with good descriptions, images and correct prices.
- Design clean, easy-to-navigate shop and product pages.
- Set up and thoroughly test online payment.
- Configure delivery methods and charges, clearly shown at checkout.
- Rely on accurate stock so the shop shows what is really available.

## My take

An online store on Odoo works best when you use its connection to the rest of the business: products, stock, sales and
accounting all in step. Good product data, a clean shop, working payment and clear delivery are the essentials, and
accurate stock ties it together. Get these right and you have a store that sells smoothly and stays in step with your
operations automatically.
