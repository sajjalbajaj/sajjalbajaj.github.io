Guessing what your customers or employees think is risky. Asking them directly, and actually listening, is
far better. Odoo Surveys makes gathering and understanding feedback simple. Continuing the series on Odoo
Marketing, here is surveys.

## Build surveys easily

Odoo lets you create surveys with different question types, multiple choice, ratings, open text and more, so
you can ask the right kind of question for what you want to learn. Building a survey is quick, and you can
shape it to be short and focused, which gets you more responses.

## Many uses

Surveys are versatile. Use them for customer satisfaction, product feedback, event feedback, employee
engagement, or even as quizzes and assessments. Because it is one flexible tool, you can gather feedback
across many parts of the business from the same place.

## Share them where your people are

You can share surveys by link, email or on your website, so you reach respondents wherever they are. And
because Odoo connects your data, you can target the right audience using the contacts you already have.

## Understand the responses

Collecting answers is only useful if you can make sense of them. Odoo helps you analyse responses, so you
can see the patterns and turn raw answers into insight. That is the difference between having feedback and
actually using it.

## Feedback that leads to action

The value of a survey is what you do with it. Because the results live in Odoo alongside your customers and
operations, it is easier to connect feedback to action: follow up unhappy customers, act on common requests,
and track whether changes help over time.

## Practical tips

- Keep surveys short and focused to get more responses.
- Choose question types that match what you actually want to learn.
- Share surveys where your audience already is.
- Act on what you learn, and close the loop with respondents where you can.

## My take

Surveys turn opinion and guesswork into real feedback you can act on. Odoo makes them easy to build, share
and analyse, and keeps the results connected to the rest of your business. Ask good questions, listen
properly, and act, and feedback becomes a genuine driver of improvement.
