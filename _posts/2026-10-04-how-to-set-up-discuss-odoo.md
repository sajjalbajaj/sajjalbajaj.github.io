Team communication scattered across separate chat apps, email and messages means conversations live apart from the work
they are about. Odoo Discuss brings messaging into the same system as everything else. Setting it up well keeps talk and
work together. Continuing this series of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is Discuss.

## Set up your channels

Start by setting up channels for how your team communicates: by team, by project, by topic. Channels keep conversations
organised, so discussions about one thing stay together. A sensible set of channels is the foundation, because it
determines whether Discuss feels tidy and useful or becomes a jumble of mixed conversations.

## Use direct messages and groups

Alongside channels, Discuss supports direct messages and small group chats for quick, personal conversations. Setting
people up to use these for one-to-one and small-group talk keeps the main channels focused. Having the right place for
each kind of conversation is what makes team communication flow naturally.

## Connect conversations to your work

The real advantage of Discuss being part of Odoo is that communication connects to records. Notifications and discussions
about a specific customer, order or task can happen in context. Setting up and using this connection means conversations
are not divorced from the work; they sit right alongside the thing being discussed.

## Get notifications right

Discuss handles a lot of Odoo's notifications, so set these up sensibly: enough to keep people informed, not so many that
they tune out. Getting the balance right is what keeps notifications useful. Too many and people ignore them all; too few
and things get missed. A thoughtful setup keeps the signal strong.

## Keep it focused on work

Discuss is a work tool, and it stays valuable when it is used for work communication rather than becoming a distraction.
Encourage using it for what helps the team get things done. Kept focused, it becomes the natural place to communicate
about work, precisely because it lives where the work does.

## Practical tips

- Set up channels by team, project or topic to keep talk organised.
- Use direct messages and groups for personal and small-group chat.
- Connect conversations to the records they concern.
- Tune notifications so they inform without overwhelming.
- Keep Discuss focused on work communication.

## My take

Discuss earns its place by keeping team communication in the same system as the work, so conversations happen in context
rather than in a separate app. Organised channels, the right use of direct messages, connection to records and
well-tuned notifications are what make it work. It will not replace every chat tool for everyone, but for talking about
the work itself, being right there alongside it is a real advantage.
