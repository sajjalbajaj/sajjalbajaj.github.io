Your people are your biggest asset, and their information is scattered across contracts, spreadsheets and
inboxes in most businesses. The Odoo Employees app pulls it into one place. This starts a short series on
Odoo HR and People.

## One home for people data

The Employees app is the central record for everyone in your organisation: contact details, job position,
department, manager, contract information and documents. Instead of hunting through folders and
spreadsheets, you have one organised directory of your team.

## Organisation structure

Odoo lets you set departments, managers and reporting lines, so the structure of your organisation is
clear and navigable. New joiners slot into the right place, and everyone can see who does what and who
reports to whom.

## The foundation for the rest of HR

Here is why the Employees app matters beyond being a directory: it is the foundation the other HR apps
build on. Time off, attendances, appraisals, payroll and expenses all draw on these employee records. Set
your people up well here, and the rest of HR has solid ground to stand on.

## Documents and contracts

Employee documents and contract details live with the employee record, so the information you need is
attached to the person it concerns. That keeps HR organised and makes it easier to stay on top of renewals,
reviews and compliance.

## Connected to the whole business

Because Employees sits inside Odoo, it connects to the rest of the system. The same people appear in
projects, timesheets, sales and more, so your team is represented consistently everywhere, not re-entered
app by app.

## Practical tips

- Get your employee records complete and accurate early, they underpin all the other HR apps.
- Set up departments and reporting lines so the structure is clear.
- Keep contracts and key documents attached to each employee record.
- Maintain the records as people join, move and leave, so HR data stays trustworthy.

## My take

The Employees app is the quiet foundation of Odoo HR. It is not glamorous, but getting your people data
organised in one connected place makes everything else, leave, attendance, appraisals, payroll, simpler and
more reliable. Start here and build out.
