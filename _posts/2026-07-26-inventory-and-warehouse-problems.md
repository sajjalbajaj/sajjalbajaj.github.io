Inventory and warehouse problems rarely announce themselves. They show up quietly as tied-up
cash, missed shipments and frustrated customers. After implementing ERP for businesses that
live and die by their stock, here are the problems I see most, and what actually causes them.

## Inventory problems that quietly cost money

- **Inaccurate stock counts.** The system says one thing, the shelf says another. Once people
  stop trusting the numbers, everything downstream suffers.
- **Stockouts.** You run out of a fast mover and lose the sale, or worse, the customer.
- **Overstock and dead stock.** Cash sits on the shelf in items that are not moving, quietly
  draining working capital.
- **No real-time visibility.** Different teams keep their own lists, so nobody has one true
  picture of what is on hand.
- **Manual entry errors.** Every re-typed number is a chance to be wrong, and those errors
  compound over time.
- **Weak forecasting.** Ordering on gut feel leads to stockouts and overstock at the same time.
- **No traceability.** Without lot or serial tracking, recalls and quality issues become a
  nightmare.

## Warehouse problems that slow you down

- **Slow, error-prone picking.** Paper pick lists and memory lead to wrong items, wrong
  quantities and unhappy customers.
- **Stock is hard to find.** With no clear location or bin structure, staff waste time hunting.
- **No barcode scanning.** Manual confirmation is slow, and mistakes slip through.
- **Wrong shipments and returns.** Every picking error becomes a return, a refund and a dent
  in trust.
- **Poor space utilization.** Without put-away rules, space is wasted and popular items end up
  far from packing.
- **No multi-warehouse visibility.** Stock is spread across locations with no single view, so
  transfers and availability become guesswork.

## The common root cause

Almost every one of these traces back to the same few causes: spreadsheets that do not scale,
disconnected systems that do not talk to each other, dirty or duplicated master data, and no
single real-time source of truth. The symptoms differ, but the disease is usually the same.

## How ERP and a WMS fix this

A well-configured ERP with warehouse management turns this around:

- Real-time stock across every location, visible to everyone.
- Reorder rules that prevent stockouts and overstock automatically.
- Barcode and mobile scanning that make every movement fast and accurate.
- Locations, bins and put-away rules, so nothing gets lost.
- Lot and serial tracking for full traceability.
- One connected system where a single scan updates stock, orders and accounting at once.

Odoo, for example, handles all of this out of the box, tied straight to sales, purchase and
accounting.

## My take

The software is not the hard part. Most inventory and warehouse problems are really process
and data problems wearing a technology costume. Fix the process, clean the data, then let the
ERP enforce the good habits. Do that, and the quiet losses stop.
