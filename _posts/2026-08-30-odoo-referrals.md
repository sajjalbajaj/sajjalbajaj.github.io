Some of the best hires come through the people you already trust. Your employees know talented people, and
a referral often means a better cultural fit and a faster hire. Odoo Referrals makes tapping that easy. To
close the series on Odoo HR and People, here is referrals.

## Your team as a hiring channel

Referrals turns your employees into an active hiring channel. It makes it simple for staff to see open
positions and refer people they know, so your open roles reach beyond job boards into the trusted networks
of your existing team.

## Easy for employees to take part

The value of a referral programme depends on people actually using it, so it has to be easy and engaging.
Odoo Referrals is designed to make participating simple and even a little fun, so employees are encouraged to
put good people forward rather than seeing it as a hassle.

## Rewards and recognition

Referral programmes work best when there is something in it for the referrer. Odoo supports rewarding
employees for successful referrals, which motivates participation and recognises the value they add by
bringing in great people.

## Connected to recruitment

A referred candidate does not sit in a separate silo. Referrals connects to the Recruitment app, so a
referred person enters the same hiring pipeline as any other candidate and is tracked through to a hire. The
referral channel feeds straight into your normal, organised process.

## Why it is worth it

Referral hires are often faster, cheaper and better-fitting than other channels, because they come
pre-vetted by someone who knows both the person and your business. A simple, rewarding referral programme is
one of the highest-value, lowest-cost recruitment tools you have.

## Practical tips

- Keep open roles visible to your team so referrals are easy to make.
- Make participating simple, friction kills referral programmes.
- Offer meaningful rewards and recognise successful referrals.
- Let referred candidates flow into your normal recruitment pipeline.

## My take

Referrals is a smart way to turn the networks of people you already trust into a hiring advantage. By making
it easy and rewarding, and connecting it to recruitment, Odoo helps you tap one of the best sources of great
hires. For a growing team, that is well worth switching on.
