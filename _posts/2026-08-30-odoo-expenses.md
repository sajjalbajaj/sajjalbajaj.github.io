Expense claims are a classic source of friction: paper receipts, chased forms, slow reimbursement and
manual entry into the accounts. Odoo Expenses smooths the whole flow. Continuing the series on Odoo HR and
People, here is expenses.

## Easy submission

Employees can submit expenses with the receipt attached, so a claim is captured quickly rather than
building up as a pile of paper. Making submission easy means claims actually get entered promptly and
accurately, instead of being reconstructed weeks later.

## Clear approvals

Expense claims go to managers to approve in one place, with the details and receipts attached. Approvals
are quick and consistent, and every decision is recorded, so there is no ambiguity about what was approved
and when.

## Straight into accounting

This is the key efficiency: approved expenses flow into your accounting rather than being re-typed. The cost
is recorded and reimbursement is handled through the same system, so finance is not left re-entering what HR
already collected. One flow from claim to books.

## Faster reimbursement

Because the process is digital and connected, employees get reimbursed faster, which people genuinely
appreciate. A slow, manual expense process is a small but real morale drain, and speeding it up is an easy
win.

## A clear record

Every claim, receipt and approval is recorded, giving you an organised history for reporting and
compliance. When you need to see what was spent, by whom and on what, it is there rather than scattered
across drawers and inboxes.

## Practical tips

- Encourage prompt submission with receipts attached, while details are fresh.
- Keep approvals quick so reimbursement is not held up.
- Let approved expenses flow into accounting rather than re-keying them.
- Use the records for clear reporting and easier compliance.

## My take

Expenses is a small app that removes a disproportionate amount of hassle. Easy submission, quick approval
and a direct line into accounting mean less paperwork, faster reimbursement and happier employees. It is
exactly the kind of everyday friction an ERP should quietly remove.
