The point of running your shop on Odoo is that the website is connected to the rest of the business. Yet many stores
throw that advantage away with a few common mistakes, and lose sales in the process. Continuing this series on common
Odoo mistakes, here is Website and eCommerce.

## Treating the website as separate

The biggest mistake is running the Odoo website as if it were a standalone site that happens to sit near your ERP. The
whole value is the connection: orders flow into sales, stock updates live, customers are the same records everywhere.
Set it up to work as one system, not as a disconnected shopfront bolted on the side.

## Neglecting product data

An online shop is only as good as its product information. Thin descriptions, missing images, wrong prices and
unmanaged stock all cost sales directly. Because the same product data drives the shop, keeping it rich and accurate is
not busywork; it is the difference between a store that sells and one that frustrates.

## Ignoring stock accuracy online

Selling things you do not have, or hiding things you do, both come from poor stock accuracy feeding the website. When
inventory is reliable, the shop shows the truth: what is available, what is not. Get the inventory right and the
storefront becomes trustworthy on its own.

## Overlooking SEO basics

A beautiful shop nobody can find earns nothing. Skipping the basics, page titles, descriptions, clean URLs, sensible
structure, means search engines struggle to surface your products. Odoo gives you the tools; the mistake is not using
them. A little SEO discipline is what turns a shop into a shop with visitors.

## Complicating the checkout

Every extra step, every forced account, every surprise at checkout loses buyers. Stores often over-engineer the
purchase flow and wonder why carts get abandoned. Keep checkout simple, clear and honest about costs, and more of the
people who wanted to buy actually do.

## Practical tips

- Run the website as part of your connected system, not a separate site.
- Keep product data rich: good descriptions, images and correct prices.
- Rely on accurate stock so the shop shows the truth.
- Cover the SEO basics so people can find your products.
- Keep checkout short, clear and free of surprises.

## My take

eCommerce on Odoo shines when it is genuinely connected and let down when it is treated as a separate site with
neglected data. Rich products, accurate stock, basic SEO and a clean checkout are not advanced tactics; they are the
fundamentals. Get them right and the connected shop does what it promises: sells more with less manual work behind it.
