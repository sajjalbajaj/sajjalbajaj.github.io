Subcontracting means part of your production happens somewhere else, which is efficient but hard to
control: your materials sit at a partner's site, and you still have to keep stock and costs accurate.
Continuing the series on industry problems and how ERP solves them, here is subcontracting.

## Problem: components sent out go off the books

When you send raw materials to a subcontractor, they physically leave your warehouse but are still yours.
Tracked badly, they simply vanish from your records.

**How Odoo solves it:** Odoo's subcontracting handling keeps the components you send to a subcontractor
visible as your stock, so nothing goes missing off your books while it is being worked on.

## Problem: finished goods coming back are hard to reconcile

You expect finished products back for the materials you sent, but matching what returns to what went out
is fiddly without a system.

**How Odoo solves it:** because the subcontracted product is defined with its bill of materials, Odoo
knows what components a finished item consumed, so receiving the finished goods reconciles cleanly.

## Problem: you do not know the true cost

With materials, subcontractor charges and your own overhead spread around, the real cost of a
subcontracted product is unclear.

**How Odoo solves it:** bringing the component cost and the subcontracting charge together gives you a
clear, accurate cost for each finished product.

## Problem: managing multiple subcontractors

Different products, different partners, different arrangements, tracking it all by email and spreadsheet
is chaos.

**How Odoo solves it:** recording each subcontractor and the products they make keeps every arrangement
organised in one place, so you always know who is making what.

## Problem: stock and production still need to connect

Subcontracting should not create a blind spot between your inventory and your production.

**How Odoo solves it:** because subcontracting sits inside the same system as inventory, purchasing and
sales, the outsourced step stays connected to the rest of your operation.
