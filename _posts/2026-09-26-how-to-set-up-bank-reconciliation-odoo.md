Bank reconciliation is where your accounting meets reality: it matches what your books say against what your bank
statement says. Setting it up well makes keeping your books accurate quick rather than painful. Continuing this series
of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is bank reconciliation.

## Connect or import your bank

The first step is getting your bank transactions into Odoo. In many places you can connect your bank so transactions
flow in automatically, which is the smoothest option. Where that is not available, you import statements as files.
Either way, the goal is that Odoo has your actual bank activity to match against.

## Understand what reconciliation does

Reconciliation is the process of matching each bank transaction to the corresponding entry in your books, an invoice you
raised, a bill you paid. When everything matches, your records agree with your bank, and you can trust your cash
position. It is the routine check that keeps accounting honest.

## Let Odoo suggest matches

Odoo tries to match bank transactions to your existing invoices and bills automatically, proposing matches for you to
confirm. This is where a lot of the time saving lives: instead of hunting for each match by hand, you review and approve
what the system suggests. Good setup makes these suggestions more accurate.

## Set up reconciliation rules

For recurring transactions, bank fees, regular payments, you can set up rules that tell Odoo how to handle them
automatically. This turns repetitive reconciliation into something the system largely does for you, leaving you to
handle only the exceptions. A little rule setup pays off every time you reconcile.

## Reconcile regularly

The most important habit is to reconcile often, not once a quarter in a panic. Regular reconciliation catches small
discrepancies while they are easy to understand and fix. Books that are reconciled frequently stay trustworthy; books
left for months become a stressful puzzle. Build the routine and it stays easy.

## Practical tips

- Connect your bank feed, or import statements, so Odoo has real activity.
- Understand reconciliation as matching your books to your bank.
- Review and confirm Odoo's suggested matches rather than matching by hand.
- Set up rules for recurring transactions like fees and regular payments.
- Reconcile regularly so discrepancies stay small and easy to fix.

## My take

Bank reconciliation is one of those routines that is painless when set up well and miserable when neglected. A connected
or imported bank feed, good match suggestions, sensible rules and the habit of reconciling regularly keep your books in
step with reality. Get this running smoothly and you always know your true cash position, which is exactly what
accounting is for.
