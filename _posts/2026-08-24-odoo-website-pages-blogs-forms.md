Once your Odoo site exists, the value comes from filling it with the right content: pages that inform, a
blog that attracts visitors, and forms that turn those visitors into leads. Continuing the series on Odoo
Website and eCommerce, here is how to build it out.

## Pages: the backbone of your site

Pages are the core of any website: home, about, services or products, contact, and whatever else your
business needs. In Odoo you create and edit them with the same drag-and-drop builder, so adding a new
page or updating an existing one is quick and needs no developer.

The key is clarity: each page should have a clear purpose and a clear next step for the visitor, whether
that is to learn more, get in touch, or buy.

## A blog to attract visitors

A blog is one of the most effective ways to bring people to your site from search. By publishing useful
articles on topics your customers care about, you give search engines more reasons to send you visitors,
and give those visitors a reason to trust you.

Odoo includes a blog you can run from within the same system, so your content, your site and your business
all live together. Regular, genuinely helpful posts compound over time into steady traffic.

## Forms that capture leads

A form is where a visitor becomes a contact. A contact form, an enquiry form, a newsletter signup, each is
a chance to start a relationship.

The powerful part in Odoo is that forms connect straight into the rest of the system. A submitted enquiry
can create a lead in your CRM automatically, so nothing lands in an inbox and gets forgotten. The person
who filled it in is now in your pipeline, ready to be followed up.

## It all connects

This is the theme that runs through Odoo Website: the site is not separate from your business. Pages
present your offer, the blog attracts an audience, and forms feed real leads into your CRM. One connected
system, from first visit to first conversation.

## Practical tips

- Give every page a clear purpose and an obvious next step.
- Publish useful blog content regularly, not just occasionally.
- Connect your forms to your CRM so leads are captured automatically.
- Follow up new leads quickly, speed is one of the biggest factors in winning them.

## My take

Pages, blog and forms are how a website earns its keep: informing, attracting and converting. Odoo lets
you build all three yourself, and connects the leads straight into your CRM, so your site becomes a real
part of how you win customers, not just a brochure.
