Support requests arrive through email, phone, website forms, chat and messaging apps. When each channel creates a separate queue, requests can be missed or answered twice. Odoo Helpdesk brings tickets into team pipelines with stages, assignments and service measures. Clinics, veterinary businesses and skincare brands can use the same foundation while keeping their different customer information and escalation rules clear.

For the general setup, see [how to set up Helpdesk in Odoo](/blog/2026/09/28/how-to-set-up-helpdesk-odoo/). This guide focuses on routing requests for specific industries.

## Create teams around the work

Avoid a single queue called “Support” for every request. A clinic might separate appointment and billing questions from provider follow-up. A veterinary group might route booking requests, pharmacy availability and post-visit administration separately. A skincare brand may separate order tracking, returns, product usage questions and quality complaints.

For each team, define who can see tickets, who assigns them, what counts as urgent and when a request moves to another team. Odoo Helpdesk supports configurable ticket stages and assignment. Its channels can include email aliases, live chat and website forms when enabled. Use clear categories so staff can see the difference between a missed appointment and a product safety concern.

## Connect the ticket to the next action

A ticket should have a defined outcome. An order issue may need a return or refund. A product fault may need a lot number, photo, quality review and replacement. A clinic request may need an appointment rescheduled or an invoice corrected. Odoo's documented Helpdesk workflows can connect after sales services such as product returns, repairs or field service tasks, depending on installed apps and configuration.

Record the minimum information needed to resolve the request. Link a ticket to the relevant customer, order, product or appointment where appropriate. Use internal notes for staff context, but do not place sensitive information in a public website description or customer facing message.

## Protect clinical and personal data

For healthcare and veterinary teams, define a bright line between administrative support and clinical care. A ticket can route a request to a qualified person, but it should not be treated as a substitute for a clinical record, treatment plan or emergency process. Do not encourage customers to send sensitive health details through a public form or an unreviewed messaging channel.

Review who can open attachments, export tickets, use chatter and view customer history. Establish a retention policy and a procedure for misdirected messages. Staff need a clear escalation instruction for urgent symptoms, adverse reactions or animal emergencies. That instruction should tell them to use the organization's approved clinical pathway rather than offer diagnosis in a support reply.

## Set response goals and learn from tickets

Use service level agreements for defined priorities and measure overdue tickets, first response time, resolution time, reopened cases and customer ratings. Separate operational targets by request type. A password reset and a product quality complaint should not be treated as equivalent work.

Review ticket themes regularly. Repeated questions may indicate that a website article, booking confirmation or product label is unclear. Odoo's Help Center can connect knowledge articles, forums and eLearning content when the corresponding features are enabled. Keep instructions accurate and assign an owner to review them.

Pilot the workflow with a small team. Test an email, a web form, a reassignment, a refund, a closed ticket and an escalated ticket. Measure whether the request reached the right person with enough context and without exposing information to the wrong audience.
