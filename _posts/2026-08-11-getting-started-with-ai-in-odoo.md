If you run Odoo, you may already be sitting on AI features you have never switched on. The question is
not "should we use AI," it is "where do we start so it actually pays off?" Here is the practical path I
would take.

## Start with a real, repetitive task

The mistake is switching on everything at once and hoping. Instead, pick one task that is repetitive,
time-consuming and low-risk if it needs a correction. That is where AI earns its keep fastest, and
where a win builds confidence for the next step.

## The four best places to begin

In my experience, these give the quickest return in Odoo:

- **Invoice digitisation (Accounting).** If your team keys in vendor bills by hand, turn on document
  digitisation. Odoo reads the invoice and fills in the details; your team just reviews. This is often
  the single biggest time saver.
- **Lead scoring (CRM).** If sales are spreading effort evenly across every lead, let Odoo rank them by
  likelihood to convert, so the best opportunities rise to the top.
- **Content generation (Website / Marketing).** If writing product descriptions or copy is a
  bottleneck, use AI to draft first versions your team then polishes.
- **Thread summaries and helpdesk drafts.** If people lose time catching up on long conversations or
  writing similar replies, lean on summaries and suggested responses.

Pick the one that matches your loudest pain. Just one, to begin.

## Keep a human in the loop

This is the golden rule. AI should draft and prepare; a person should approve anything that matters:
money, customer-facing messages, important records. Set it up so AI removes the tedious first pass, not
the final judgement. That keeps you safe and builds trust in the tool.

## Clean your data first

AI is only as good as what it reads. Duplicate customers, messy product data or half-filled records
lead to confident nonsense. Before you lean on AI in an area, tidy the data it depends on. This one
step separates AI that helps from AI that embarrasses you.

## Mind the provider and compliance

Odoo can work with more than one AI provider, such as OpenAI or Google Gemini. That flexibility matters
if you have data residency or compliance requirements, so decide up front which provider and settings
fit your rules, especially in regulated industries.

## Measure the payoff

Do not run on vibes. Before you start, note how long the task takes today. After a few weeks with AI,
measure again: time saved, error rate, how the team feels about it. If it is a clear win, expand to the
next area. If not, adjust or try a different starting point.

## A simple rollout plan

1. Pick one high-value, low-risk task.
2. Clean the data it relies on.
3. Turn on the relevant Odoo AI feature, with a human approving the output.
4. Measure the result over a few weeks.
5. If it works, move to the next area and repeat.

## My take

Getting value from AI in Odoo is not about flipping every switch. It is about choosing one real
problem, feeding it clean data, keeping a person in control, and measuring the result. Do that and AI
stops being a buzzword and starts quietly giving your team hours back. Then you widen the circle.
