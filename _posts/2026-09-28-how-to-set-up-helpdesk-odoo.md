Good support keeps the customers you worked hard to win, and a helpdesk that captures every request and moves it along
is how you deliver it. Setting up Odoo Helpdesk well is what makes support organised rather than chaotic. Continuing this
series of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is the helpdesk.

## Set up your helpdesk teams

Start by creating your helpdesk team or teams. A small business might have one; a larger one might split by product,
region or type of issue. Each team has its own tickets and members, so setting them up to match how you actually handle
support keeps things clear and makes reporting meaningful.

## Decide how tickets come in

Tickets can arrive through several channels: an email address, a website form, a live chat, or entered by your team. Set
up the channels your customers actually use, so their requests land in the helpdesk automatically rather than sitting in
someone's personal inbox. Capturing every request is the foundation of good support.

## Set up your stages

Like a pipeline, tickets move through stages: new, in progress, waiting, solved. Set stages that reflect how your team
actually works a ticket. Clear stages make the state of every request obvious and stop tickets from silently stalling,
which is the most common way support lets customers down.

## Assign ownership

Every ticket needs someone responsible for it. Set up assignment, manually or automatically, so no ticket sits ownerless.
Clear ownership is what turns a queue of requests into worked, accountable tickets. Without it, everyone assumes someone
else has picked it up, and customers wait.

## Add a knowledge base and consider SLAs

To handle common questions efficiently, set up a knowledge base so answers can be reused rather than rewritten. If you
promise response or resolution times, set up service level agreements so the system helps you meet them. Both turn a
reactive helpdesk into a professional, dependable support operation.

## Practical tips

- Create helpdesk teams that match how you handle support.
- Set up the ticket channels your customers actually use.
- Define clear stages so no ticket silently stalls.
- Assign every ticket an owner, manually or automatically.
- Add a knowledge base and SLAs for efficient, dependable support.

## My take

A well-set-up helpdesk is what makes support a strength rather than a scramble. Teams that match your reality, the right
intake channels, clear stages and firm ownership ensure every request is captured and moved along. Add a knowledge base
and SLAs and you have support that customers can rely on, which is one of the quieter but most powerful ways to keep them.
