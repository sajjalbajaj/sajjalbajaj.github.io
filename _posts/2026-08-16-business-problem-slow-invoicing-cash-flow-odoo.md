Next in the series on business problems and how Odoo solves them: slow invoicing, and the way it quietly
strangles your cash flow.

## The problem

The work is done, but the invoice goes out days later, because someone has to build it by hand from the
order. It has an error, so it gets queried and delayed. Nobody is quite tracking who has paid and who
has not. Overdue invoices sit there because chasing them is awkward and keeps getting put off.

Meanwhile, the money you have already earned is sitting outside your bank account, when you need it
inside.

## Why it hurts

Cash flow, not profit, is what keeps a business alive day to day. Every day between doing the work and
getting paid is a day your cash is tied up. Slow invoicing stretches that gap. Errors stretch it
further. Poor follow-up stretches it further still. Plenty of profitable businesses get into trouble
simply because the cash comes in too slowly.

## How Odoo helps

Odoo shortens the distance between finishing the work and getting paid:

- **Invoices generate straight from orders**, with the right products, quantities and prices already
  filled in, so they go out fast and accurate.
- **Send instantly** from Odoo, as a clean professional invoice, the moment the work is done.
- **Track every invoice** through draft, sent, paid and overdue, so you always know who owes you what.
- **Follow up overdue invoices** as a simple routine, with reminders, rather than an awkward
  afterthought.

Faster, accurate invoices that are easy to track and chase mean money arrives sooner and disputes are
rarer. That is a direct, measurable improvement to your cash flow.

## Where to start

Start generating invoices from your orders rather than building them by hand, and send them the moment
work is complete. Then make reviewing your overdue list a weekly habit. Those two changes alone usually
pull cash in noticeably faster.

## My take

Invoicing is not just admin, it is cash flow, and cash flow is survival. The gap between doing the work
and getting paid is one of the most important numbers in your business, and slow manual invoicing makes
it worse. Odoo closes that gap by making invoices fast, accurate, tracked and easy to chase. For many
businesses, that is the difference between a comfortable month and a tense one.
