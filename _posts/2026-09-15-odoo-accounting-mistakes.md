Accounting is the one area where mistakes are least forgiving, because the numbers have to be right and auditors will
notice if they are not. The good news is that the common Odoo accounting mistakes are well known and avoidable.
Continuing this series on common Odoo mistakes, here is Accounting.

## Rushing the chart of accounts

The chart of accounts is the backbone of your books, and rushing it causes trouble for years. Too few accounts and you
cannot see detail; too many and reporting becomes a mess. Set it up thoughtfully, ideally with your accountant, before
you start posting, because reworking it later is genuinely hard.

## Not reconciling the bank regularly

Bank reconciliation is where your records meet reality. Teams that let it slip end up with books that drift from the
actual bank balance, and catching up later is miserable. Reconcile regularly and little discrepancies get caught while
they are still small and easy to explain.

## Getting taxes and fiscal positions wrong

Tax setup is fiddly and easy to get wrong, and wrong tax means wrong invoices and awkward corrections. Fiscal positions
that automatically apply the right tax by customer type or region are there to help; used properly they prevent a whole
category of errors. Take the time to set them up correctly.

## Editing instead of correcting

A classic mistake is trying to fix a posted entry by editing it, rather than using a proper correction like a credit
note. Accounting needs an audit trail, and editing history away breaks it. Learn the right way to correct things, and
your books stay clean and defensible.

## Ignoring analytic accounting

Businesses that want to know profitability by project, department or product often skip analytic accounting and then
wonder why they cannot answer those questions. A little setup here unlocks the management reporting that makes the
numbers actually useful, rather than just compliant.

## Practical tips

- Design the chart of accounts carefully, with your accountant, before posting.
- Reconcile the bank regularly so records never drift far from reality.
- Set up taxes and fiscal positions properly to prevent invoice errors.
- Correct entries the right way; never edit history away.
- Use analytic accounting if you want profitability by project or department.

## My take

Accounting punishes shortcuts more than any other area, but the mistakes are predictable and preventable. A
well-designed chart of accounts, regular reconciliation, correct tax setup and proper corrections keep your books clean
and audit-ready. Get the foundations right and Odoo accounting becomes a source of confidence rather than anxiety.
