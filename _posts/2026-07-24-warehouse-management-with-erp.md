A warehouse is where your ERP meets the real, physical world. You can have perfect stock
numbers on screen, but if picking is slow and errors are common, your customers still feel it.
Good warehouse management turns that chaos into flow.

## Inventory management vs warehouse management

Inventory management answers "how much do we have, and where". Warehouse management goes
further: it runs the physical operations of receiving, put-away, picking, packing and shipping
accurately and efficiently. A warehouse management system (WMS) is the layer that orchestrates
all of this.

## What a WMS actually does

- **Organizes stock by locations and bins**, so anything can be found fast.
- **Uses barcode scanning** to make every movement accurate and traceable.
- **Applies put-away rules** so goods land in the right place on arrival.
- **Drives picking strategies** (batch, wave, zone) to cut walking and save time.

## Barcode scanning changes everything

Manual, paper-based picking is slow and error-prone. Barcode or mobile scanning confirms the
right item, quantity and location at each step, which slashes mistakes and speeds up
fulfillment.

## Picking and rotation strategies

Rotation matters for many businesses: FIFO for general stock, FEFO where expiry dates count
(food and pharma). Batch and zone picking group work smartly, so staff move less and ship more.

## Multi-warehouse and internal routes

As you grow, you may run several warehouses and move stock between them. ERP handles
multi-warehouse operations and internal transfer routes, so movements stay tracked and stock
stays accurate everywhere.

## How ERP and Odoo help

An ERP with built-in warehouse management, like Odoo, connects the floor to the rest of the
business. A single scan on the warehouse floor updates stock, orders and accounting instantly,
with no double entry.

## My take

Map your real physical flow first: how goods arrive, get stored, get picked and ship. Then
configure the WMS to match it, add scanning, and keep it simple. That is how a warehouse goes
from chaos to control.
