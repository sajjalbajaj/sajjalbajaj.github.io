Trading and distribution live or die on margin, stock and speed: buy well, hold the right inventory,
sell fast and keep costs tight. Continuing the series on industry problems and how ERP solves them, here
is trading and distribution.

## Problem: margins are thin and easily lost

In trading, you make money on the gap between buying and selling, and that gap is easily eroded by hidden
costs and sloppy pricing.

**How Odoo solves it:** with purchase costs, pricelists and margins visible in one system, you can see
your real margin on every product and protect it, rather than discovering erosion after the fact.

## Problem: stock is spread across warehouses

Holding stock in several locations without a clear view means overstock in one place, shortages in
another, and cash tied up in the wrong goods.

**How Odoo solves it:** multi-warehouse inventory gives one accurate view of stock everywhere, so you
balance it sensibly and always know what you can actually sell.

## Problem: pricing is complex

Different customers, quantities and deals mean pricing is rarely one number, and managing that by hand is
error-prone.

**How Odoo solves it:** pricelists apply the right price automatically by customer, quantity or
promotion, so quoting is fast, consistent and correct.

## Problem: the real cost of goods is unclear

Freight, duties and handling all add to what a product really costs, and ignoring them makes your margins
a fiction.

**How Odoo solves it:** landed cost handling adds those extra costs to your inventory valuation, so the
cost of goods reflects reality and your margins are honest.

## Problem: fast-moving stock needs tight control

In distribution, stock turns quickly, and losing track means missed sales or dead inventory.

**How Odoo solves it:** real-time inventory with reordering rules keeps fast movers in stock and flags
slow ones, so your cash stays in the goods that actually sell.
