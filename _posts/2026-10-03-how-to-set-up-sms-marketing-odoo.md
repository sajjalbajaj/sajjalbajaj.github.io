Text messages get read, often within minutes, which makes SMS a powerful channel for timely, important messages. Odoo
SMS Marketing lets you send them from the same contact data as everything else. Setting it up well, and using it with
restraint, is the key. Continuing this series of practical Odoo how-to guides, here is SMS Marketing.

## Set up SMS sending

Sending SMS relies on a messaging service, which in Odoo typically works through in-app purchase credits, you buy
credits and spend them per message. Set this up so you can send, and understand the cost per message. Knowing the cost
matters, because unlike email, each SMS has a real price attached.

## Build your audience

As with email, build your audience from your contacts, using segmentation to target the right people. SMS is
particularly personal and direct, so targeting matters even more. Set up your lists thoughtfully, and make sure you have
the right to text these people. The right audience is what makes SMS welcome rather than intrusive.

## Write short, clear messages

SMS is short by nature, so every word counts. Set up your messages to be clear, immediate and useful, with a single
obvious point or action. There is no room for waffle in a text. A well-written SMS respects the reader's time and gets
its message across in a glance, which is exactly what the channel is for.

## Use it for the right things

SMS works best for timely, valuable messages: an order is ready, an appointment reminder, a genuinely important offer.
Set up your SMS use around these high-value moments rather than routine marketing. Because texts are so direct, using
them well means using them sparingly, for things the recipient will actually be glad to get.

## Respect frequency and consent

The fastest way to ruin SMS as a channel is to overuse it or text people who did not agree to it. Set clear limits on
frequency, honour opt-outs, and only message people who have consented. Treating SMS with this respect is what keeps it
effective, because the moment it feels like spam, people tune out or unsubscribe.

## Practical tips

- Set up SMS sending and understand the per-message cost.
- Build a targeted, permission-based audience.
- Write short, clear messages with a single obvious point.
- Use SMS for timely, high-value moments, not routine blasts.
- Respect frequency and consent to keep the channel effective.

## My take

SMS Marketing is a high-impact channel precisely because texts get read, which makes it powerful and easy to abuse. The
winning approach is restraint: a permission-based audience, short and clear messages, used sparingly for genuinely
valuable moments. Set up that way, SMS reaches customers when it really matters, without wearing out its welcome. Used
carelessly, it burns the channel fast.
