For any business that sells or manages time, knowing where the hours actually go is essential, for billing,
for pricing and for knowing which work pays. Odoo Timesheets makes capturing that simple. Continuing the
series on Odoo Services and Project, here is timesheets.

## Simple time logging

Timesheets lets people log the time they spend, against projects and tasks. The easier it is to log time,
the more reliably it gets done, so Odoo keeps it quick, including logging against the very tasks people are
already working on in Project.

## Time tied to real work

Because timesheets connect to projects and tasks, logged hours are tied to actual work, not just a vague
daily total. That means you can see how much time a project, a task or a client really consumed, which is
the basis for almost every useful decision about services work.

## The foundation for billing

For businesses that bill by time, timesheets are the source of truth for invoicing. Hours logged can flow
into billing, so you invoice accurately for the work done, without reconstructing it from memory. We will
cover that flow later in the series.

## Insight into where time goes

Even if you do not bill by the hour, knowing where time goes is gold. It shows which work is efficient and
which quietly eats hours, which clients are demanding, and where to improve. You cannot manage what you do
not measure, and timesheets measure your most finite resource.

## Low friction is the key

The whole thing only works if people actually log time, so keeping it low-friction matters more than any
feature. Odoo aims to make logging quick and part of the flow, so timesheets reflect reality rather than a
Friday-afternoon guess.

## Practical tips

- Make logging time quick and part of the daily routine.
- Log against real projects and tasks, not just totals.
- Use the data to bill accurately and to understand profitability.
- Watch where time actually goes, and act on what it reveals.

## My take

Timesheets are the quiet foundation of a healthy services business. Logged well, they tell you where your
most valuable resource, time, really goes, and they feed accurate billing and real profitability insight.
Keep logging simple, and the data becomes something you genuinely run the business on.
