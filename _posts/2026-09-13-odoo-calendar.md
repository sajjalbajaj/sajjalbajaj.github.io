A calendar is only useful if it reflects what you are actually doing, and a calendar cut off from your work quickly
drifts out of date. Odoo Calendar sits inside the system where the work happens. Continuing this run through more Odoo
apps, here is Calendar.

## Your schedule in one place

Calendar gives you a clear view of your meetings, appointments and scheduled activities. Rather than juggling several
places to know what your day looks like, you have one calendar that shows it, shared and personal events together.

## Connected to your work

The real advantage is connection. Meetings and activities scheduled against a customer, an opportunity or a project
show up on your calendar and stay linked to that record. So a meeting is not just a block of time; it carries the
context of what it is about and who it is with.

## Syncs with Google and Outlook

Nobody wants to abandon the calendar they already live in. Odoo Calendar can sync with Google Calendar and Outlook, so
events flow both ways and you are not maintaining two separate schedules. You keep using what you know while Odoo stays
in step.

## Shared calendars and availability

Because calendars can be shared, you can see colleagues' availability when arranging something, which makes scheduling
across a team far less of a back-and-forth. Finding a time that works becomes a glance rather than a chain of emails.

## Part of the connected system

Like the rest of Odoo, Calendar is not a standalone tool but a view into your work over time. The meetings, calls and
activities you schedule elsewhere in the system land here, so your calendar reflects reality instead of being a
separate thing to maintain.

## Practical tips

- Schedule activities against records so meetings carry their context.
- Sync with Google or Outlook so you keep one real calendar, not two.
- Use shared availability to arrange team meetings without the email chain.
- Let the calendar reflect work from across Odoo rather than duplicating it.

## My take

Calendar is quietly one of the more useful connective apps, because a schedule tied to your actual work stays accurate
in a way a standalone calendar never does. With Google and Outlook sync it does not force you to switch, and with
records linked to events your day carries real context. Simple, but genuinely helpful.
