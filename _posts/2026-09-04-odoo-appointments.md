Scheduling a meeting by email is a slow dance: what times suit you, no not then, how about this, and round
again. Odoo Appointments removes that friction by letting people book your time directly. Continuing the
series on Odoo Services and Project, here is appointments.

## Self-service booking

Appointments lets clients and prospects book a slot with you online, choosing from times you have made
available. No back-and-forth emails, no double-bookings. They pick a slot that works, and it is booked.

## Based on real availability

Because Appointments connects to your calendar, people book against your actual availability. You set the
rules, when you are bookable, how long slots are, and Odoo only offers times that genuinely work, so you
stay in control of your schedule.

## A professional first impression

A smooth, self-service booking experience reflects well on your business. It is convenient for the client
and signals that you are organised, which matters, especially for the first interaction with a prospect.

## Fewer no-shows, less admin

Automated confirmations and reminders help reduce no-shows, and because booking is self-service, you spend
far less time arranging meetings manually. That is time back for the actual work.

## Connected to the rest of Odoo

Because it is part of Odoo, a booking can tie into your calendar, your contacts and the rest of your
workflow. The person booking is in your system, and the meeting is part of your organised day, not a
separate app you have to reconcile.

## Practical tips

- Set clear availability rules so only suitable times are offered.
- Share your booking link where prospects and clients will use it.
- Use confirmations and reminders to cut no-shows.
- Let bookings flow into your calendar and contacts automatically.

## My take

Appointments removes one of the most tedious small frictions in business: arranging meetings. Letting people
book real, available time themselves is convenient for them, professional for you, and frees you from the
scheduling back-and-forth. A small tool that quietly saves a lot of time.
