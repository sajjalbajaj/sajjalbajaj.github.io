A beautiful website is worth little if no one finds it. Search engines are how most people discover a
business, so being findable matters. The good news is that Odoo Website has practical SEO tools built in.
Continuing the series on Odoo Website and eCommerce, here is SEO.

## Titles and meta descriptions

Two of the most important on-page SEO elements are the page title and the meta description, the headline
and summary that show up in search results. Odoo lets you set these per page, so each page can present a
clear, compelling title and description to both Google and the people deciding whether to click.

Getting these right, unique and accurate for each page, is one of the simplest, highest-value things you
can do for search.

## Clean, readable URLs

Descriptive URLs help both search engines and visitors understand what a page is about. Odoo produces
sensible, readable page addresses rather than cryptic strings, which is a small but genuine SEO plus.

## Sitemaps and indexing

Search engines find your pages more reliably when you give them a map. Odoo generates a sitemap of your
site automatically, so search engines can discover and index your pages, including new ones as you add
them.

## Fast, mobile-friendly pages

Speed and mobile-friendliness are real ranking factors, and they affect whether visitors stay. Because
Odoo's pages are responsive and built on a solid foundation, you start from a good place on both, rather
than fighting a slow, clunky site.

## Content is still what wins

Tools help, but the thing that truly earns rankings is useful, well-structured content that answers what
people are searching for. Odoo gives you the builder and the SEO fields; filling them with genuinely
helpful content, clear headings, honest answers, real value, is what makes the difference.

## Practical tips

- Write a unique, accurate title and meta description for every important page.
- Use clear headings and helpful content that matches what people search for.
- Make sure your pages are fast and look right on mobile.
- Let Odoo generate the sitemap, and submit your site to Google Search Console.

## My take

SEO is not magic, and Odoo will not do it all for you, but it gives you the practical tools that matter:
per-page titles and descriptions, clean URLs, an automatic sitemap and fast, mobile-friendly pages. Pair
those with genuinely useful content and you give your site a real chance to be found.
