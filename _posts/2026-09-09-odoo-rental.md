Renting out products is a different beast from selling them: the same item goes out and comes back, again and
again, and you have to track availability, timing and condition. Continuing this run through more Odoo apps,
here is Rental.

## Renting, not just selling

The Rental app is built for businesses that rent products rather than sell them: equipment, tools, vehicles,
event gear, and more. Instead of a one-time sale, you manage items that go out for a period and return, which
needs its own way of thinking about stock and time.

## Availability over time

The key challenge in rental is knowing what is free, when. Rental helps you see availability across time, so
you can tell a customer whether an item is free for the dates they want, and avoid double-booking the same
unit. Time-based availability is exactly what ordinary stock tracking does not handle.

## Rental pricing

Rental pricing works differently too, often by duration: per hour, day, week or longer. Odoo lets you price
rentals by period, so quotes and orders reflect how long the customer keeps the item, rather than a single
sale price.

## Pickup and return

A rental has two moments that matter: it goes out, and it comes back. Odoo helps you manage both, so you know
what is currently out with customers, what is due back, and when. That keeps your fleet of rentable items
under control instead of scattered and forgotten.

## Connected to the rest

Because Rental sits in Odoo, it connects to your customers, invoicing and the rest of the business. A rental
order can be quoted, confirmed, delivered and invoiced through the same flow as everything else, with rental
income landing in your accounts.

## Practical tips

- Track availability over time so you never double-book a unit.
- Price rentals by duration to match how customers use the product.
- Manage both pickup and return so you always know what is out.
- Let rental orders and income flow through your normal Odoo processes.

## My take

Rental solves the specific problem selling-focused systems cannot: the same item, out and back, over time.
Odoo gives you availability, duration pricing, and pickup and return in one place, connected to the rest of
the business. For any rental operation, that is the difference between control and chaos.
