Ordering lunch for an office sounds trivial until you are the one collecting everyone's order, chasing money and
phoning the same vendor every day. Odoo Lunch turns that little daily scramble into something orderly. Kicking off
another run through more Odoo apps, here is Lunch.

## Employees order for themselves

Instead of one person taking down everyone's order, Lunch lets employees browse the available vendors and place their
own order from within Odoo. They pick what they want, it is recorded, and nobody has to remember whether Priya wanted
the salad or the sandwich.

## Manage your vendors and menus

You set up the vendors your office orders from and their menus, so people are choosing from real, current options.
When a vendor changes their prices or dishes, you update it once and everyone sees the right menu. It keeps the whole
thing tidy rather than relying on a dog-eared paper menu pinned to the wall.

## Keep the money straight

Lunch tracks orders and costs, so who owes what is clear rather than a source of low-level friction. Whether the
company subsidises meals or staff pay their own way, having it recorded means the money side does not turn into an
awkward weekly reconciliation.

## A small perk, handled well

Office meals are a genuine perk, and handling them smoothly makes the workplace a little nicer. Lunch takes something
that is often a minor daily annoyance and makes it effortless, which is exactly what a good small app should do.

## Part of your HR picture

Because Lunch lives in Odoo alongside your HR and employee data, it fits into the wider workplace rather than being a
separate spreadsheet someone maintains. It is one more everyday thing handled inside the same connected system.

## Practical tips

- Set up your regular vendors and their menus so choices are always current.
- Let employees place their own orders to save one person the daily round-up.
- Use the order and cost tracking to keep the money side clear.
- Treat it as part of your workplace tools, not a side spreadsheet.

## My take

Lunch is a small, cheerful app that solves a real if minor daily headache. By letting people order for themselves from
managed vendors and keeping the costs straight, it removes the friction from a nice workplace perk. Not a
business-critical app, but a genuinely pleasant one, and a good example of Odoo covering the little things too.
